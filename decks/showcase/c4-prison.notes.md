# C4 Prison: Notes

Part of the [archetype showcase](ARCHETYPES.md). Shared rules and power band
live there.

## Constraints

- Mono-white plus colorless artifacts. Every card taxes, locks, or dodges
  the locks.
- No exclusions.

## Primer

### Game Plan

Lock pieces make every spell cost more, limit how many spells each player
can cast, and tax attacks. Your efficient creatures dodge the locks and win
while the opponent struggles to act. Wins around turns 7 to 9.

### Key Synergies

- Mind Stone and the cheap creatures let you operate under your own taxes,
  while the opponent has no such cushion.
- Creatures and Mutavault are not hit by Thorn of Amethyst, Thalia, or
  Deafening Silence, so the locks mostly hit the opponent's spells.
- Armageddon after you have a board and a Mind Stone lets you rebuild first.

### Mana

- **Mind Stone:** a colorless mana rock that ramps you through your own
  taxes and can be sacrificed late to draw a card.
- **Plains (18):** the white mana for every spell in the deck.
- **Field of Ruin:** a colorless land that can be sacrificed to destroy a
  nonbasic land, which answers an opposing utility or dual land.
- **Mutavault:** a land that becomes a 2/2 creature for {1}; it also serves
  as a threat that sorcery-speed removal and sweepers miss.

### Taxes

- **Sphere of Resistance:** all spells cost {1} more for every player.
  Play it early; the Mind Stones keep you ahead of it.
- **Thorn of Amethyst:** noncreature spells cost {1} more, which hurts
  Storm, control, and combo decks while your creatures are untaxed.
- **Thalia, Guardian of Thraben:** a first-strike creature that also taxes
  noncreature spells by {1}; it is both a tax and a clock.
- **Lodestone Golem:** a large artifact creature that makes nonartifact
  spells cost {1} more; cast it on turn 3 with a Mind Stone.

### Locks

- **Chalice of the Void:** choose X when you cast it; it counters any spell
  with that mana value. Set it to 1 or 2 against cheap decks. Remember it
  also counters your own spells of that value.
- **Deafening Silence:** each player can cast only one noncreature spell
  each turn, which blanks combo turns. Your creatures are unaffected.
- **Archon of Emeria:** a flying creature that limits each player to one
  spell per turn; it slows both players, so deploy it when ahead.
- **Ghostly Prison:** creatures cannot attack you unless their controller
  pays {2} for each one, so it blunts aggro and go-wide decks.

### Threats

- **Brimaz, King of Oreskos:** a vigilance creature that makes a Cat token
  each time it attacks or blocks; a threat that also defends.
- **Adeline, Resplendent Cathar:** grows with your creature count and makes
  Human tokens when you attack; the strongest threat in the deck.
- **Mutavault:** an extra attacker that dodges sorcery-speed removal.

### Removal and Reset

- **Path to Exile:** exiles a creature at instant speed for {W}. Save it for
  the creature that breaks through your locks; the opponent gets a land.
- **Armageddon:** destroys all lands. Cast it only when you have more
  creatures or a Mind Stone and the opponent has none; note that your own
  Mutavault and Field of Ruin are lands and are destroyed too.

### Mulligan

Keep hands with 2 to 4 lands and either a one- or two-mana lock piece
(Thorn, Sphere, Thalia) or a Mind Stone. Mulligan hands with no lands or
only expensive cards such as Lodestone Golem and Archon.

### Turn Guide

1. Turn 1: play a land, then Thalia if you have it; otherwise pass.
2. Turn 2: Mind Stone, Sphere of Resistance, or Thorn of Amethyst.
3. Turn 3: Brimaz, Adeline, or Ghostly Prison; Chalice for 1 if the
   opponent is cheap and fast.
4. Turn 4: Lodestone Golem or Archon of Emeria, plus a cheap lock piece.
5. Turns 5-6: if you lead on board and they are short on lands, Armageddon,
   then attack with whatever survived.
6. Turns 7-9: keep attacking; Path the one creature that stands in the way.

### Matchups/Cautions

- **Good against:** Storm, Prowess, and other spell-heavy combo decks.
- **Struggles against:** big creature decks, and artifact removal such as
  Toolbox's answers, which breaks Chalice, Sphere, and Mind Stone.
- **Caution:** your own taxes slow you down; do not stack too many locks
  without a threat on board.
