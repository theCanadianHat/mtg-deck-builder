# M6 Enchantress: Notes

Part of the [archetype showcase](ARCHETYPES.md). Shared rules and power band
live there.

## Goals

- Show the midrange lane that draws a card whenever an enchantment is cast
  and builds into an overwhelming board.
- Wins around turns 7 to 9 with Sigil tokens, Starfield, or a big creature.

## Constraints

- Selesnya (GW). Every card is an enchantment or rewards enchantments.
- No exclusions.

## Primer

### Game Plan

Land an enchantress engine by turn 2, then chain cheap enchantments so every
spell replaces itself. Once an engine sticks, ramp Auras and cheap
enchantments feed it, and defensive enchantments keep you safe. Wins around
turns 7 to 9 with Sigil tokens, Starfield, or a big creature.

### Key Synergies

- **Engines stack:** every enchantment cast triggers each of Argothian
  Enchantress, Enchantress's Presence, Satyr Enchanter, and Sythis.
- **Ramp that draws:** Utopia Sprawl and Wild Growth are Auras, so they
  ramp and trigger every engine.
- **Enchantment count:** Sphere of Safety, Serra's Sanctum, Sanctum Weaver,
  Destiny Spinner, and Ancestral Mask all scale with how many you control.
- **Sigil of the Empty Throne:** every enchantment you cast makes a 4/4
  flying Angel, so it turns the engines into a board.

### Draw Engines

- **Argothian Enchantress:** draws a card when you cast an enchantment. It
  has shroud, so it is hard to remove.
- **Enchantress's Presence:** an enchantment engine that draws on every
  enchantment spell you cast. Cast it early.
- **Satyr Enchanter:** a creature that draws whenever you cast an
  enchantment spell.
- **Sythis, Harvest's Hand:** gains 1 life and draws a card on each
  enchantment spell.
- **Setessan Champion:** gets a counter and draws whenever an enchantment
  enters under your control, so it also counts Auras that enter without
  being cast.
- **Eidolon of Blossoms:** draws a card whenever an enchantment enters under
  your control.

### Ramp

- **Utopia Sprawl:** an Aura on a Forest that adds an extra mana. It is a
  turn 1 play that triggers any engine you have.
- **Wild Growth:** an Aura that adds an extra green mana from a land. Play
  it after an engine.
- **Sanctum Weaver:** taps for mana equal to your enchantment count, so it
  powers a large turn.
- **Serra's Sanctum:** taps for one white per enchantment you control.

### Defense

- **Sphere of Safety:** creatures cannot attack you unless their controller
  pays for each attacker, based on your enchantment count. Cast it before the
  opponent attacks, as a wall.
- **Oblivion Ring:** exiles a nonland permanent until it leaves. It answers
  any problem and is a cheap enchantment for the engines.

### Payoffs

- **Sigil of the Empty Throne:** a 4/4 flying Angel for every enchantment
  you cast. It is the main win condition.
- **Starfield of Nyx:** returns an enchantment from the graveyard each
  upkeep, and with five enchantments turns your non-Aura enchantments into
  creatures.
- **Ancestral Mask:** an Aura that gives +2/+2 for each other enchantment
  on the battlefield, so one creature can win quickly.
- **Destiny Spinner:** protects your creature and enchantment spells from
  counterspells, and turns a land into an X/X attacker, where X is your
  enchantment count.

### Mana

- **Temple Garden:** a dual land that makes both colors.
- **Windswept Heath:** fetches Forest, Plains, or Temple Garden.
- **Serra's Sanctum:** see Ramp. It makes a large amount of white mana late.
- **Forest and Plains:** the basic lands. Green is the main color, and
  Utopia Sprawl needs a Forest.

### Mulligan

Keep hands with 2 to 4 lands and either an engine creature or a ramp Aura.
Utopia Sprawl into an engine is the best start. A hand with no enchantments
or engine is a mulligan.

### Turn Guide

1. Turn 1: land and Utopia Sprawl, or hold for an engine.
2. Turn 2: Argothian Enchantress, Satyr Enchanter, or Enchantress's
   Presence, or Wild Growth.
3. Turn 3: Sythis, Setessan Champion, or Eidolon of Blossoms, then cheap
   enchantments with any spare mana.
4. Turn 4: Oblivion Ring, Sphere of Safety, or Sigil of the Empty Throne.
5. Turns 5 to 9: chain enchantments to draw through the deck, make Angels
   with Sigil, and attack with Ancestral Mask, Starfield, or Destiny Spinner.

### Matchups/Cautions

- **Good against:** creature decks and slow control, since Sphere and your
  card draw out-grind them.
- **Struggles against:** fast aggro and enchantment removal.
- **Caution:** count enchantments before casting Sphere of Safety or
  tapping Serra's Sanctum. Guard your engine creature when you can.
