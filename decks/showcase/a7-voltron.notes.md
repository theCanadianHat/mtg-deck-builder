# A7 Voltron: Notes

Part of the [archetype showcase](ARCHETYPES.md). Shared rules and power band
live there.

## Goals

- Play one hexproof creature that removal cannot target, then stack auras
  on it until it wins in three or four swings.
- Wins around turns 5 to 6.

## Constraints

- Selesnya (GW). Every creature carries auras or rewards them.
- No exclusions.

## Primer

### Game Plan

Play a hexproof creature on turn 1, then add one or two auras every turn.
Targeted removal cannot touch the creature, and Umbras protect it from
destruction. Three or four swings win the game. Wins around turns 5 to 6.

### Key Synergies

- **Hexproof carrier:** Slippery Bogle and Gladecover Scout cannot be
  targeted by the opponent, so auras are safe from spot removal.
- **Aura card draw:** Kor Spiritdancer and Sram replace the cards you
  spend on auras.
- **Aura and enchantment count:** Ethereal Armor, All That Glitters, and
  Ancestral Mask grow with the number of Auras and enchantments.
- **Umbra armor:** Spider Umbra and Hyena Umbra save the creature from
  destruction, which helps against edicts and sweepers.

### Creatures

- **Slippery Bogle:** a 1/1 hexproof creature for {G/U}. Cast it on turn 1.
- **Gladecover Scout:** a 1/1 hexproof creature for {G}. A second
  hexproof carrier.
- **Kor Spiritdancer:** gets +2/+2 for each Aura attached to it, and draws
  a card whenever you cast an Aura spell. Cast it before the auras.
- **Sram, Senior Edificer:** draws a card whenever you cast an Aura,
  Equipment, or Vehicle spell. Two copies; a card-draw engine for auras.

### Auras

- **Ethereal Armor:** +1/+1 for each enchantment you control, and first
  strike. Every Aura, including itself, adds to it.
- **Rancor:** +2/+0 and trample. It returns to your hand if put into a
  graveyard, so it is a repeatable threat.
- **Spider Umbra:** +1/+1 and reach, with umbra armor. If the enchanted
  creature would be destroyed, remove all damage from it and destroy this
  Aura instead.
- **Hyena Umbra:** +1/+1 and first strike, with umbra armor. A second
  protection layer.
- **Daybreak Coronet:** gives +3/+3, first strike, vigilance, and lifelink.
  It can only enchant a creature that already has another Aura, so cast
  it after another Aura to race aggro.
- **All That Glitters:** +1/+1 for each artifact and enchantment you
  control. Auras count, including itself.
- **Ancestral Mask:** +2/+2 for each other enchantment on the battlefield.
  Grows with every Aura on your side, and on theirs.

### Protection

- **Blossoming Defense:** +2/+2 and hexproof until end of turn. Use it on
  Kor Spiritdancer or Sram, which lack hexproof, in response to targeted
  removal. It does nothing against sweepers or edicts.

### Mana

- **Temple Garden:** a shock land for green and white. Pay 2 life to enter
  untapped.
- **Razorverge Thicket:** a green and white land that enters untapped if you
  control two or fewer other lands. Play it early.
- **Windswept Heath:** fetches Temple Garden, Forest, or Plains.
- **Forest (4) and Plains (4):** basic lands for the two colors.

### Mulligan

Keep hands with 2 or 3 lands, a hexproof creature, and an aura. Mulligan
hands with only auras and no creature.

### Turn Guide

1. Turn 1: Slippery Bogle or Gladecover Scout.
2. Turn 2: Kor Spiritdancer, or an aura on your hexproof creature.
3. Turn 3: one or two auras, such as Rancor and Spider Umbra, and attack.
4. Turn 4: Daybreak Coronet once another Aura is on the creature, or
   Ethereal Armor, All That Glitters, or Ancestral Mask.
5. Turns 5 to 6: swing with the loaded creature for lethal.

### Matchups and Cautions

- **Good against:** decks built on targeted removal (Rock, Draw-go).
- **Struggles against:** sacrifice effects, sweepers, and chump-blocking
  token decks.
- Watch for edicts and sweepers; Umbras help against both. Do not
  overcommit auras to a creature when the opponent has a sweeper.
