# C1 Draw-go: Notes

Part of the [archetype showcase](ARCHETYPES.md). Shared rules and power band
live there.

## Goals

- Show the control lane: hold up instants, say no, win late.
- Stabilizes around turn 5 and wins around turns 10 to 12.

## Constraints

- Azorius (UW). Every nonland card is an instant or has flash, except Shark
  Typhoon, which is cycled at instant speed.
- Win conditions are flash creatures, Shark tokens, and Celestial Colonnade.
- No exclusions.

## Primer

### Game Plan

Pass the turn with mana open. Counter what you cannot answer later, and let
small threats resolve so you can remove them with Swords to Plowshares or
Path to Exile. At the end of their turn, if you did not need to counter
anything, spend the mana on Opt, Fact or Fiction, or a flash creature.
Stabilizes around turn 5 and wins around turns 10 to 12.

### Key Synergies

- **Instant speed:** nearly every spell can be cast on their turn, so you
  never waste mana.
- **Snapcaster Mage:** gives flashback to an instant or sorcery in your
  graveyard, so a counterspell or removal spell is used twice.
- **Shark Typhoon:** whenever you cast a noncreature spell, you make a
  flying Shark, so counterspells and cantrips build a board.
- **Restoration Angel:** blinks another non-Angel creature, which resets
  Snapcaster Mage or Vendilion Clique, or saves one from removal.

### Counterspells

- **Counterspell:** counters a target spell for {U}{U}. Save it for the
  spell that you cannot answer later.
- **Mana Leak:** counters a spell unless its controller pays {3}. Best
  early, before the opponent has spare mana.
- **Memory Lapse:** counters a spell and puts it on top of its owner's
  library. A tempo play that makes them redraw it.
- **Absorb:** counters a spell and you gain 3 life. Use it against burn or
  an aggro deck.
- **Archmage's Charm:** choose one: counter a spell, a player draws two
  cards, or gain control of a nonland permanent with mana value 1 or less.
  Flexible; use it as a counter or at the end of the turn to draw.
- **Cryptic Command:** choose two: counter a spell, return a permanent to
  its owner's hand, tap all creatures your opponents control, or draw a
  card. Counter and draw is the common mode.

### Draw and Selection

- **Opt:** scry 1, then draw a card. A cheap play to smooth draws at the
  end of their turn.
- **Fact or Fiction:** reveal the top five cards; the opponent splits them
  into two piles and you choose one. A strong end-of-turn card.

### Removal

- **Swords to Plowshares:** exile a creature and its controller gains life
  equal to its power. A one-mana answer.
- **Path to Exile:** exile a creature and its controller may search for a
  basic land. Use it on a threat that you cannot counter.
- **Settle the Wreckage:** exiles all attacking creatures target player
  controls, and they search for basic lands. Cast it during their attack to
  answer a wide board.

### Finishers

- **Restoration Angel:** a 3/4 flash flyer that can blink a non-Angel
  creature. Cast it at the end of their turn or to ambush an attacker.
- **Vendilion Clique:** a 3/1 flash flyer that looks at a hand and may
  bottom a card. Cast it at the end of their turn to check their hand.
- **Snapcaster Mage:** a flash creature that gives an instant or sorcery in
  your graveyard flashback this turn. Cast it to reuse a counterspell.
- **Shark Typhoon:** cast it as an enchantment to make a Shark for each
  noncreature spell, or cycle it for {X}{1}{U} at instant speed to make an
  X/X flying Shark. Use cycling to answer a threat or as a flash finisher.
- **Celestial Colonnade:** a land that becomes a 4/4 flying vigilance
  creature until end of turn for {3}{W}{U}. It is a win condition that
  dodges sorcery-speed sweepers.

### Mana

- **Hallowed Fountain:** a shock land for white and blue. Pay 2 life to
  enter untapped.
- **Flooded Strand:** fetches Hallowed Fountain, Island, or Plains.
- **Mystic Gate:** a filter land. Pay a hybrid white-blue mana to produce
  two mana of white, blue, or one of each. Helps with color requirements.
- **Island (8) and Plains (3):** basic lands. Blue is the main color.

### Mulligan

Keep hands with 3 or 4 lands, at least one counterspell or cantrip, and
some interaction. Hands with Opt and Counterspell are strong. Mulligan
hands with no lands or only creatures.

### Turn Guide

1. Turns 1 to 2: lands. Opt at the end of their turn if you have no
   counter to hold up.
2. Turn 3: hold Counterspell or Mana Leak. Remove small threats later
   with Swords or Path.
3. Turn 4: hold up two or three mana for Absorb, Memory Lapse, or Archmage's
   Charm. Cast Fact or Fiction at the end of their turn if you were not
   forced to counter.
4. Turn 5: Cryptic Command, Settle the Wreckage, or a flash creature.
5. Turns 6 to 12: flash in Restoration Angel, Vendilion Clique, or Shark
   Typhoon, and activate Celestial Colonnade to win.

### Matchups and Cautions

- **Good against:** combo and slow decks that rely on big spells.
- **Struggles against:** cheap threats that come down before counters are
  up (Burn, Prowess, Aether Vial in Tribal).
- Counter what you cannot answer later. Let small threats resolve and
  remove them with Swords or Path.
