# C5 Mill: Notes

Part of the [archetype showcase](ARCHETYPES.md). Shared rules and power band
live there.

## Constraints

- Dimir (UB). Every card mills or protects the mill plan.
- No exclusions.

## Primer

### Game Plan

Ignore their life total. Put their library into their graveyard; they lose
when they cannot draw. Crabs start the clock on turn 1, big mill spells
finish it, and cheap interaction keeps you alive. Wins around turns 6 to 8.

### Key Synergies

- Landfall crabs plus fetch lands: a fetch land entering and the land it
  fetches are two land drops, so two mill triggers per fetch.
- Archive Trap is free after the opponent searches their library, and your
  fetch-heavy opponents' own fetch lands can enable it.
- Drown in the Loch gets stronger as the graveyard fills, so your mill
  also powers your removal and counterspell.

### Landfall Engines

- **Hedron Crab:** one-mana blue creature; each land you play mills three
  cards from the target player. Play it on turn 1.
- **Ruin Crab:** same landfall mill-three trigger; a second crab doubles the
  mill per land drop.

### Mill Spells

- **Glimpse the Unthinkable:** mills ten from the target player for two
  mana; a cheap, efficient chunk of mill.
- **Maddening Cacophony:** mills a large chunk, and when kicked also mills
  half their library, so save it for a later turn with extra mana.
- **Fractured Sanity:** a large mill spell that can also be cycled for a
  smaller mill when you need mana or a land instead.
- **Mind Funeral:** reveals cards from the top of their library until four
  lands are found and mills all of them; strong against land-heavy decks.
- **Tasha's Hideous Laughter:** each opponent exiles cards from the top until
  the exiled cards total mana value 20 or more. It exiles rather than
  mills, so it doesn't feed Drown in the Loch, but it still shrinks the
  library. Best against decks with a high curve.
- **Archive Trap:** mills thirteen; cast it for free if the opponent
  searched their library this turn, otherwise it is expensive.

### Interaction

- **Drown in the Loch:** counters a spell or destroys a creature when its
  mana value is at most the number of cards in the opponent's graveyard.
  Hold it until the graveyard is large enough.
- **Fatal Push:** cheap instant removal for a small creature, such as a
  one- or two-mana attacker that threatens to race you.
- **Counterspell:** hard counter for a key spell; hold {U}{U} up when the
  opponent has a haymaker or a way to stop your crabs.

### Mana

- **Watery Grave:** a dual land that can be fetched; counts as a land drop.
- **Polluted Delta:** fetches Watery Grave, Island, or Swamp; each
  crack is two landfall triggers with a crab out.
- **Misty Rainforest:** fetches an Island or Watery Grave in this deck;
  the same double-landfall value as Delta.
- **Fabled Passage:** fetches a basic land (two landfall triggers with a
  crab out); the fetched land enters untapped with four or more lands.
- **Island (6) and Swamp (3):** basic lands that give landfall triggers
  and color for the spells.

### Mulligan

Keep hands with 2 to 4 lands, ideally a crab and a way to cast it on turn 1
or 2. A fetch land plus a crab is great. Mulligan hands with no lands or
hands that are all expensive mill spells.

### Turn Guide

1. Turn 1: land and Hedron Crab; if you have a fetch, play it next turn.
2. Turn 2: Ruin Crab or another crab, then crack the fetch for double mill.
3. Turn 3: keep making land drops; cast Glimpse the Unthinkable or hold
   up Counterspell or Fatal Push for a key threat.
4. Turns 4-5: mill spells such as Maddening Cacophony, Fractured Sanity, or
   Mind Funeral. Look for a turn with opponent fetches for a free Archive Trap.
5. Turns 6-8: finish the library. Count their library each turn.

### Matchups/Cautions

- **Good against:** slow control and Lifegain, which cannot race a library.
- **Struggles against:** graveyard decks such as Dredge, Reanimator, and
  Graveyard value, which like a full graveyard and can even gain from your
  mill.
- **Caution:** do not mill your opponent's reanimation targets into their
  graveyard before Drown in the Loch or other answers are ready.
