# A6 Artifact Aggro: Notes

Part of the [archetype showcase](ARCHETYPES.md). Shared rules and power band
live there.

## Goals

- Dump a hand of free and cheap artifacts by turn 2.
- Use cards that scale with artifact count to hit for huge amounts.
- Wins around turns 4 to 5.

## Constraints

- Mostly colorless, with light blue and red. Every card is an artifact or
  rewards artifacts.
- No exclusions.

## Primer

### Game Plan

Empty your hand fast. Every artifact you play makes the next one cheaper
through affinity, and each one powers up Cranial Plating, Signal Pest, and
Steel Overseer. Put Plating on a flyer (Ornithopter, Inkmoth Nexus) and
swing. Wins around turns 4 to 5.

### Key Synergies

- **Affinity:** Frogmite, Myr Enforcer, and Thoughtcast cost {1} less for
  each artifact you control, so they are often free.
- **Plating and evasion:** Cranial Plating on Ornithopter or Inkmoth
  Nexus makes a flying threat that grows with every artifact.
- **Battle cry and counters:** Signal Pest and Steel Overseer reward a
  wide board of artifact creatures.
- **Ravager:** Arcbound Ravager sacrifices artifacts to grow, and moves
  its counters when it dies, so it blanks removal.
- **Artifact lands:** Darksteel Citadel, Seat of the Synod, and the lands
  that animate count as artifacts or help cast spells.

### Free and Cheap Artifacts

- **Ornithopter:** a 0/2 flying creature for {0}. The best Plating carrier
  and a free artifact for affinity.
- **Memnite:** a 1/1 creature for {0}. A free artifact and a body.
- **Signal Pest:** a 0/1 with battle cry that can only be blocked by flying
  or reach creatures. It pumps every attacker with each artifact swing.

### Payoffs

- **Arcbound Ravager:** enters with a +1/+1 counter (modular). Sacrifice an
  artifact to put a +1/+1 counter on it. In response to removal, sacrifice
  the targeted artifact to Ravager.
- **Steel Overseer:** {T}: put a +1/+1 counter on each artifact creature you
  control. A repeatable pump for a board of artifact creatures.
- **Cranial Plating:** gets +1/+0 for each artifact you control. Equip it
  to the best evasive creature.
- **Etched Champion:** a 2/2 that has protection from all colors with
  metalcraft (three or more artifacts). It is a hard-to-answer attacker
  once the board is out.

### Affinity Creatures and Card Draw

- **Frogmite:** a 2/2 with affinity for artifacts. Usually free to cast on
  turn 2.
- **Myr Enforcer:** a 4/4 with affinity for artifacts. A large body for
  little or no mana once you have an artifact board.
- **Thoughtcast:** draws two cards, with affinity for artifacts. Cast it
  when you run out of cards.

### Removal

- **Galvanic Blast:** 2 damage to any target for {R}, or 4 damage with
  metalcraft. Use it to remove a blocker or finish.

### Mana

- **Darksteel Citadel:** an indestructible artifact land. It counts for
  affinity and Cranial Plating.
- **Seat of the Synod:** an artifact land that taps for blue. It adds to
  the artifact count.
- **Glimmervoid:** taps for any color, but you sacrifice it at the end
  step if you control no artifacts. Makes red for Galvanic Blast.
- **Spire of Industry:** taps for colorless, or any color if you control an
  artifact. A colored source.
- **Inkmoth Nexus:** a land that becomes a 1/1 flying infect artifact
  creature for {1}. A Plating carrier that dodges sorcery-speed removal.

### Mulligan

Keep hands with 2 or 3 lands and several cheap artifacts. A hand with
Ornithopter or Memnite and an affinity card is strong. Mulligan hands with
few artifacts or no way to deploy.

### Turn Guide

1. Turn 1: Ornithopter, Memnite, and a one-mana artifact.
2. Turn 2: cast Frogmite, Myr Enforcer, or Thoughtcast, which are
   cheaper with each artifact in play. Add Cranial Plating.
3. Turn 3: equip Plating to Ornithopter or Inkmoth Nexus and attack.
   Use Arcbound Ravager to protect the board from removal.
4. Turns 4 to 5: add Steel Overseer, Signal Pest, and Etched Champion,
   then use Galvanic Blast to finish.

### Matchups and Cautions

- **Good against:** slow decks that cannot block flyers.
- **Struggles against:** sweepers and artifact removal (Toolbox's Qasali
  Pridemage and Reclamation Sage).
- In response to removal, sacrifice the targeted artifact to Ravager.
