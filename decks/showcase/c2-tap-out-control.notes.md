# C2 Tap-out Control: Notes

Part of the [archetype showcase](ARCHETYPES.md). Shared rules and power band
live there.

## Goals

- Unlike Draw-go, this deck spends its mana on its own turn: sweepers to
  reset the board, sorcery-speed card draw, and a few huge finishers.
- Stabilizes around turn 5 and wins around turns 10 to 12.

## Constraints

- Orzhov (WB). Every card is removal, a sweeper, card draw, or a finisher.
- No exclusions.

## Primer

### Game Plan

Let the opponent overcommit, then sweep. A two-for-one sweeper is the goal;
a four-for-one wins the game. Use spot removal on the threat that would kill
you before a sweeper, draw cards at sorcery speed, and land a finisher only
once the board is clear.

### Key Synergies

- **Sweepers and card draw:** Read the Bones, Night's Whisper, and
  Phyrexian Arena keep your hand full so you can sweep more than once.
- **Spot removal and sweepers:** cheap removal handles the one threat that
  matters, so you sweep when the opponent has overcommitted.
- **Recursion:** Sun Titan and Elspeth Conquers Death return your best
  permanents from the graveyard.
- **Planeswalkers on a clear board:** Elspeth, Sun's Champion and The
  Wandering Emperor take over once the sweeper resolves.

### Sweepers

- **Wrath of God:** destroys all creatures, and they cannot be
  regenerated. A clean reset on turn 4.
- **Damnation:** destroys all creatures like Wrath of God. A second copy of
  the same effect.
- **Day of Judgment:** destroys all creatures. The third copy of the same
  effect.
- **Sunfall:** exiles all creatures and gives you an Incubator token with a
  counter for each creature exiled. Use it against recursive creatures or
  indestructible ones.

### Spot Removal

- **Fatal Push:** destroys a creature with mana value 2 or less, or 4 or
  less with revolt. Use it on an early threat.
- **Path to Exile:** exiles a creature, and its controller searches for a
  basic land. Use it on a large threat.
- **Vindicate:** a sorcery that destroys any permanent. Use it on a
  planeswalker or a key permanent.
- **Utter End:** exiles a nonland permanent. A flexible answer to anything.
- **Shriekmaw:** destroys a nonartifact, nonblack creature when it enters,
  and can be evoked for a cheaper cost. It leaves a body if you hard-cast
  it.

### Card Advantage

- **Read the Bones:** scry 2, draw two cards, and lose 2 life. A good turn-3
  play.
- **Night's Whisper:** draw two cards and lose 2 life for {1}{B}. A cheap
  way to refill.
- **Phyrexian Arena:** at your upkeep you draw an extra card and lose 1
  life. Cast it early and when you are not under pressure.

### Finishers

- **Elspeth, Sun's Champion:** +1 makes three 1/1 Soldiers, and -3 destroys
  all creatures with power 4 or greater. Cast it on a clear board.
- **Sun Titan:** a 6/6 vigilance that returns a permanent with mana value 3
  or less from your graveyard when it enters or attacks. Brings back removal
  creatures or lands.
- **Ugin, the Spirit Dragon:** +2 deals 3 damage to any target, and -X
  exiles each colored permanent with mana value X or less. A one-card win
  condition at eight mana.
- **Elspeth Conquers Death:** a saga that exiles a permanent with mana
  value 3 or greater an opponent controls, then returns a creature or
  planeswalker from your graveyard. Removal and a recursion spell.
- **The Wandering Emperor:** a planeswalker with flash. -2 exiles a tapped
  creature and gains you 2 life. Cast it during combat as removal.

### Mana

- **Godless Shrine:** a shock land for white and black. Pay 2 life to enter
  untapped.
- **Marsh Flats:** fetches Godless Shrine, Plains, or Swamp.
- **Concealed Courtyard:** a white and black land that enters untapped if
  you control two or fewer other lands.
- **Caves of Koilos:** a painland for white and black. Taps for colorless
  with no damage.
- **Scoured Barrens:** a white and black land that enters tapped and gains
  1 life.
- **Plains (6) and Swamp (6):** basic lands that fill out both colors.

### Mulligan

Keep hands with 3 or 4 lands, a sweeper or removal, and a card-draw spell.
Mulligan hands with no lands or only finishers.

### Turn Guide

1. Turns 1 to 2: lands, and Fatal Push or Night's Whisper if needed.
2. Turn 3: Read the Bones, Phyrexian Arena, or spot removal.
3. Turn 4: Wrath of God or Damnation if the opponent has overcommitted.
   Otherwise, Vindicate or Utter End.
4. Turns 5 to 6: Elspeth Conquers Death, Shriekmaw, or Sun Titan.
5. Turns 7 to 12: Ugin, the Spirit Dragon or Elspeth, Sun's Champion on a
   clear board.

### Matchups and Cautions

- **Good against:** creature aggro (Tokens, Tribal, Counters).
- **Struggles against:** counterspells (Draw-go), Prison, and fast combo.
- Use spot removal on the threat that would kill you before a sweeper.
- Land a finisher only once the board is clear.
