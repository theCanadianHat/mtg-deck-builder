# T1 Flash Tempo: Notes

Part of the [archetype showcase](ARCHETYPES.md). Shared rules and power band
live there.

## Goals

- Show the tempo lane that mixes a few cheap evasive threats with
  counterspells and burn, spending mana on the opponent's turn.
- Wins around turns 5 to 7.

## Constraints

- Izzet (UR). Every card is a cheap threat, cheap interaction, or a cantrip.
- No exclusions.

## Primer

### Game Plan

Play one or two cheap, evasive threats early, then hold up counters and burn
to protect them. You win before the opponent's better cards matter. Spend
early turns deploying a threat and later turns holding mana open. Trade
efficiently, not completely: you only need to stop what would beat your
clock. Wins around turns 5 to 7.

### Key Synergies

- **Cantrips fuel the threats:** Consider, Thought Scour, and Expressive
  Iteration fill the graveyard for Murktide Regent's delve and Dragon's Rage
  Channeler's and Unholy Heat's delirium.
- **Instants and sorceries flip Delver:** the cantrips and burn give Delver of
  Secrets many targets to reveal and turn it into a flyer.
- **Interaction protects a clock:** Counterspell and Spell Pierce stop
  sweepers and removal, and Lightning Bolt kills blockers.
- **Brazen Borrower and Ledger Shredder:** both give flexible, instant-speed
  plays that fit the turns you spend holding up mana.

### Threats

- **Delver of Secrets:** flips into a 3/2 flyer when an instant or sorcery
  is revealed at your upkeep. Cantrips and burn make that likely.
- **Dragon's Rage Channeler:** surveils when you cast a noncreature spell
  and becomes a 3/3 flyer with delirium. It is a cheap, aggressive threat.
- **Ledger Shredder:** a flyer that connives whenever a player casts their
  second spell in a turn, so it grows as the game goes on.
- **Murktide Regent:** a large flyer that is cheap late with delve, and
  grows with each instant or sorcery in the exiled cards.
- **Brazen Borrower:** a 3/1 flash flyer that can block only flyers. Its
  Petty Theft side is an instant that bounces a nonland permanent an
  opponent controls, then you
  can cast the Borrower later from exile.

### Burn and Removal

- **Lightning Bolt:** deals 3 damage to any target. Kill a blocker or a
  small creature, or finish the opponent.
- **Unholy Heat:** deals 2 damage to a creature or planeswalker, or 6 with
  delirium. It is premium removal late in the game.

### Counterspells

- **Counterspell:** the hard counter for any spell. Hold it up for the
  spell that would beat your clock.
- **Spell Pierce:** counters a noncreature spell unless they pay 2. Best
  early against removal, sweepers, and combo spells.

### Cantrips

- **Consider:** looks at the top card, then draws a card. It fuels delve and
  flips Delver.
- **Thought Scour:** mills two cards and draws a card. It is the best
  cantrip for delve and delirium.
- **Expressive Iteration:** looks at three cards, takes one, bottoms one, and
  lets you play the third this turn. It is card advantage and a threat
  finder.

### Mana

- **Steam Vents:** a dual land that makes both colors.
- **Scalding Tarn:** fetches an Island or Mountain, including Steam Vents.
  It also fills the graveyard.
- **Spirebluff Canal:** a dual land that enters untapped early.
- **Island and Mountain:** the basic lands. Blue is the main color.

### Mulligan

Keep hands with 2 to 3 lands, a cheap threat, and a counterspell or cantrip.
A hand with Delver, Thought Scour, and Lightning Bolt is ideal. Mulligan
hands with no threat and no interaction.

### Turn Guide

1. Turn 1: Delver of Secrets, Dragon's Rage Channeler, or Consider.
2. Turn 2: Ledger Shredder, a second threat, or Thought Scour with mana
   open for Spell Pierce.
3. Turn 3: Brazen Borrower or Expressive Iteration, with Counterspell mana
   held up.
4. Turn 4: Murktide Regent, usually cheap with delve, or burn and a
   counterspell in the same turn.
5. Turns 5 to 7: attack with flyers, hold Counterspell and Spell Pierce, and
   burn the opponent out.

### Matchups/Cautions

- **Good against:** combo (Storm, Reanimator) and slow control, since
  counterspells stop their key spells.
- **Struggles against:** go-wide decks and cheap sticky creatures.
- **Caution:** do not delve away the cards that power delirium unless
  Murktide Regent will win the game.
