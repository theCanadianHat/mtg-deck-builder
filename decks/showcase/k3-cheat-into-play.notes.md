# K3 Cheat into Play: Notes

Part of the [archetype showcase](ARCHETYPES.md). Shared rules and power band
live there.

## Constraints

- Mono-red. Every card cheats in a fatty, finds one, or is a fatty.
- No exclusions.

## Primer

### Game Plan

Put a giant creature onto the battlefield without paying its cost. Sneak
Attack and Through the Breach give it haste for one big attack. Wins around
turns 4 to 5.

### Key Synergies

- Faithless Looting and Reckless Impulse find fatties and cheat effects; the
  Eldrazi shuffle your graveyard back into your library when they hit the
  graveyard, so discarding them is not a loss.
- Sneak Attack and Through the Breach give haste but sacrifice the creature
  at end of turn; annihilator and enter triggers still pay off.
- Pyretic Ritual and Mind Stone speed up the cheat spells.

### Cheat Effects

- **Sneak Attack:** pay {R} to put a creature from hand onto the
  battlefield with haste; it is sacrificed at end of turn. Repeatable
  each turn.
- **Through the Breach:** an instant that puts a creature from hand onto
  the battlefield with haste; it is sacrificed at the end step. Cast it at
  the beginning of combat.
- **Quicksilver Amulet:** pay {4} and tap to put a creature from hand
  onto the battlefield; the permanent version that keeps the creature.

### Fatties

- **Emrakul, the Aeons Torn:** annihilator 6 wrecks their board when it
  attacks; the best target. It shuffles back if it hits the graveyard.
- **Kozilek, Butcher of Truth:** annihilator 4. If it goes to the graveyard from
  anywhere, you shuffle your graveyard into your library. Its cast trigger
  (draw four) only happens if you hard-cast it.
- **Ulamog, the Infinite Gyre:** an indestructible Eldrazi with annihilator
  that also shuffles back; a large target.
- **Inferno Titan:** a cheaper fatty that deals damage when it enters or
  attacks, so cast it normally if you have six mana.
- **Terror of the Peaks:** a flying dragon that deals damage whenever
  another creature enters, so it combines with later cheats.

### Setup and Mana

- **Faithless Looting:** draws two and discards two, with flashback; discard
  Eldrazi to put them back into the library, or discard extra lands.
- **Reckless Impulse:** exiles the top two cards and lets you play them
  until the end of your next turn.
- **Pyretic Ritual:** adds {R}{R}{R} for two mana; ramps into Sneak
  Attack a turn early.
- **Mind Stone:** a colorless mana rock that can later be cashed in for a
  card.
- **Mountain (23):** the basic lands that make up the mana base.

### Removal

- **Lightning Bolt:** 3 damage for {R}; kills an early blocker or slows an
  aggressive opponent while you assemble the combo.

### Mulligan

Keep hands with a cheat effect and a fatty, or with a cheat effect and
digging to find one. Mulligan hands without lands or with only fatties.

### Turn Guide

1. Turn 1: land and Faithless Looting; Bolt an early threat if needed.
2. Turn 2: Mind Stone or Reckless Impulse; set up the cheat.
3. Turn 3-4: Sneak Attack, then pay {R} for Emrakul; or cast Through the
   Breach for an immediate attack.
4. Turn 4-5: attack with annihilator on the board; follow up with
   another fatty.

### Matchups/Cautions

- **Good against:** midrange and slow control without counterspells.
- **Struggles against:** counterspells and fast aggro.
- **Caution:** Through the Breach and Sneak Attack sacrifice the creature at
  end of turn, so attack with annihilator creatures to wreck their board;
  do not tap out if the opponent has a removal spell for an unattacked
  creature.
