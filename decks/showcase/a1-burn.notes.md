# A1 Burn: Notes

Part of the [archetype showcase](ARCHETYPES.md). Shared rules and power band
live there.

## Goals

- Show the burn lane: point every spell at the opponent's face and race.
- Wins on turn 4 or 5 if unopposed.

## Constraints

- Mono-red. Every card either deals direct damage or is a hasty, cheap threat.
- No exclusions.

## Primer

### Game Plan

Lead with a one-drop creature, then aim burn at the opponent's face unless
a blocker would cost you more damage than the spell deals. Twenty damage is
roughly seven burn spells; hasty creatures and Eidolon of the Great Revel
cover the rest. Wins on turn 4 or 5 if unopposed.

### Key Synergies

- **Spectacle:** Skewer the Critics and Light Up the Stage cost {R} once an
  opponent has lost life this turn. Attack with a haste creature or cast a
  cheap burn spell first, then cast them for one mana.
- **Cheap spells:** nearly every spell has mana value 3 or less, so they
  trigger Eidolon of the Great Revel and Monastery Swiftspear's prowess.
  Fireblast (mana value 6) does not trigger Eidolon.
- **Free finisher:** Fireblast is cast by sacrificing two Mountains, so it
  adds 4 damage without needing mana.
- **Refuel:** Light Up the Stage replaces spent cards when the opponent
  has stabilized the board.

### Threats

- **Monastery Swiftspear:** a 1/2 haste creature with prowess. Cast it on
  turn 1 and every burn spell makes it hit harder.
- **Goblin Guide:** a 2/2 haste creature for one mana. Whenever it attacks,
  the defending player reveals the top card of their library and puts it in
  their hand if it is a land. Best on turn 1; the extra land is a fair
  price for 2 damage a turn.
- **Eidolon of the Great Revel:** a 2/2 that deals 2 damage to any player
  who casts a spell with mana value 3 or less. It hurts you as well, but
  you are the one ahead in the race. Do not cast it when you are behind
  on life against a deck of cheap spells.

### Burn

- **Lightning Bolt:** 3 damage for {R} at instant speed. Aim it at face or
  kill a blocker that would stop a creature.
- **Chain Lightning:** 3 damage to any target at sorcery speed for {R}. The
  opponent may pay {R}{R} to copy it back at you, which rarely happens.
- **Lava Spike:** 3 damage to a player or planeswalker only. A pure face
  spell; it cannot remove a blocker.
- **Rift Bolt:** suspend 1 for {R}, then it deals 3 damage to any target on
  your next upkeep. Cast it by suspending on turn 1 or 2 for a cheap hit.
- **Skewer the Critics:** 3 damage to any target. Cast it for {R} with
  spectacle after the opponent has lost life this turn.
- **Fireblast:** 4 damage to any target at instant speed. Sacrifice two
  Mountains to cast it for free when it finishes the game.
- **Play with Fire:** 2 damage to any target at instant speed. If a player
  is dealt damage this way, scry 1. Use it to finish a game or to remove a
  small blocker.
- **Light Up the Stage:** exile the top two cards of your library and play
  them until the end of your next turn. Cast it for {R} with spectacle to
  refill an empty hand.

### Mana

- **Mountain (20):** the only land type. Keep at least two in play to be
  able to sacrifice them to Fireblast without stalling your development.

### Mulligan

Keep hands with 2 or 3 lands and at least two cheap spells or creatures.
A one-drop creature on turn 1 is ideal. Mulligan hands with no way to spend
mana in the first two turns or with four or more lands and no action.

### Turn Guide

1. Turn 1: Monastery Swiftspear or Goblin Guide, or suspend Rift Bolt.
2. Turn 2: attack, then cast burn after combat damage so spectacle is
   live. Add Eidolon of the Great Revel if the opponent is not on cheap
   spells.
3. Turn 3: attack again and point Lightning Bolt, Chain Lightning, or Lava
   Spike at face. Use Skewer the Critics for {R} after damage.
4. Turn 4: Light Up the Stage with spectacle if you are out of cards.
   Count lethal, including Fireblast.
5. Turn 5 and later: burn face every turn. Save Fireblast for the last 4
   damage.

### Matchups and Cautions

- **Good against:** slow decks (Turbo fog, Superfriends, Big mana) and combo.
- **Struggles against:** Lifegain and decks with cheap lifelink blockers.
- Do not use burn on creatures unless a blocker stops more damage than the
  spell would deal to the face.
