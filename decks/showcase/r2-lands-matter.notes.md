# R2 Lands Matter: Notes

Part of the [archetype showcase](ARCHETYPES.md). Shared rules and power band
live there.

## Goals

- Show the ramp lane that plays extra lands each turn and triggers landfall
  payoffs with each one.
- Wins around turns 6 to 8.

## Constraints

- Gruul (GR). Every card plays, returns, or rewards lands.
- No exclusions.

## Primer

### Game Plan

Play extra lands every turn and trigger landfall payoffs with each one.
Recursion replays sacrificed or discarded lands, so every land counts twice.
Fetch lands and Harrow give two landfall triggers each. Wins around turns 6
to 8.

### Key Synergies

- **Extra land drops:** Exploration, Azusa, Dryad of the Ilysian Grove, and
  Mina and Denn let you play several lands each turn, so each payoff
  triggers several times.
- **Life from the Loam with Seismic Assault:** discard lands for damage,
  then get them back with Loam.
- **Scapeshift with 7 or more lands:** fetches Valakut and Mountains for
  lethal damage.
- **Dryad of the Ilysian Grove:** makes your lands every basic land type, so
  every land is a Mountain for Valakut.

### Extra Land Drops

- **Exploration:** lets you play an additional land each turn. Cast it on
  turn 1 for the fastest start.
- **Azusa, Lost but Seeking:** lets you play two additional lands each turn.
- **Dryad of the Ilysian Grove:** one additional land per turn, and your
  lands are every basic land type. It fixes your mana and powers Valakut.
- **Mina and Denn, Wildborn:** one additional land per turn, and can return
  a land to your hand to give a creature trample. That replays the land for
  another landfall trigger.
- **Ramunap Excavator:** lets you play lands from the graveyard, which
  recovers fetch lands and sacrificed lands.
- **Crucible of Worlds:** the same effect as Excavator, but as an artifact.

### Ramp and Landfall Engines

- **Lotus Cobra:** adds a mana whenever a land enters, so several land
  drops in a turn pay for a large spell.
- **Harrow:** sacrifices a land to fetch two basic lands onto the
  battlefield untapped. It is two landfall triggers at instant speed.
- **Rampaging Baloths:** a 6/6 trampler that creates a 4/4 Beast whenever a
  land enters under your control. It is the best payoff for many land drops.
- **Avenger of Zendikar:** makes a 0/1 Plant for each land you control, then
  puts a counter on each Plant for each landfall.
- **Omnath, Locus of Rage:** creates a 5/5 Elemental whenever a land enters,
  and deals 3 damage when it or another Elemental dies.

### Finishers

- **Valakut, the Molten Pinnacle:** deals 3 damage to any target whenever a
  Mountain enters if you control five other Mountains.
- **Scapeshift:** sacrifices lands to fetch that many lands, so it brings
  Valakut and Mountains in for lethal.
- **Seismic Assault:** discards a land card for 2 damage to any target. It
  makes every extra land a burn spell.
- **Life from the Loam:** returns up to three lands from the graveyard to
  your hand, so Seismic Assault and sacrifice effects keep going. It also
  dredges.
- **Raging Ravine:** a land that becomes a 3/3 attacker that grows each
  attack. It is a threat that does not die to sorcery-speed removal.

### Mana

- **Wooded Foothills:** fetches a Mountain or Forest, and gives a landfall
  trigger twice.
- **Prismatic Vista:** fetches any basic land and gives a landfall trigger
  twice.
- **Stomping Ground:** a dual land that makes both colors.
- **Raging Ravine:** see Finishers. It makes both colors.
- **Mountain and Forest:** the basic lands. Mountains are what Valakut
  counts.

### Mulligan

Keep hands with 2 to 4 lands and an extra land drop or a ramp spell, such as
Exploration, Lotus Cobra, or Harrow. Mulligan hands with only payoffs and no
land drops.

### Turn Guide

1. Turn 1: land and Exploration, or Wooded Foothills with a landfall play.
2. Turn 2: Lotus Cobra, Azusa, Dryad of the Ilysian Grove, or Harrow.
3. Turn 3: Rampaging Baloths, Mina and Denn, or Avenger of Zendikar with
   extra lands.
4. Turn 4: Omnath, Ramunap Excavator, or Life from the Loam with Seismic
   Assault.
5. Turns 5 to 8: Scapeshift or Valakut with 7 or more lands, or attack with
   Baloths, Avenger, and Raging Ravine.

### Matchups/Cautions

- **Good against:** slow control and midrange, since you out-ramp them.
- **Struggles against:** land destruction (Prison) and graveyard hate.
- **Caution:** do not sacrifice lands for Harrow or Scapeshift unless you
  can replay them. Valakut needs five other Mountains.
