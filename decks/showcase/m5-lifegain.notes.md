# M5 Lifegain: Notes

Part of the [archetype showcase](ARCHETYPES.md). Shared rules and power band
live there.

## Goals

- Show the midrange lane that gains life constantly and uses payoffs that
  grow creatures or drain the opponent whenever life is gained.
- Wins around turns 6 to 7.

## Constraints

- Orzhov (WB). Every card gains life or pays it off.
- No exclusions.

## Primer

### Game Plan

Gain life many small times each turn and use payoffs that grow creatures or
drain the opponent whenever you gain life. Count triggers, not totals: three
gains of 1 beat one gain of 3. Wins around turns 6 to 7.

### Key Synergies

- **Small gains times many payoffs:** Soul Warden, Healer's Hawk, and Lunarch
  Veteran gain life often, and each gain triggers Ajani's Pridemate,
  Marauding Blight-Priest, Vito, and Archangel of Thune.
- **Lifelink as a source:** Vampire Nighthawk, Healer's Hawk, Archangel of
  Thune, and Heliod's lifelink ability all trigger payoffs on combat damage.
- **Drain payoffs stack:** Vito, Sanguine Bond, and Marauding Blight-Priest
  turn each gain into life loss for the opponent.
- **Righteous Valkyrie:** makes your team much larger once you are 7 life
  above your starting total.

### Small and Frequent Life Gain

- **Soul Warden:** gains 1 life whenever another creature enters, yours or
  the opponent's. Cast it on turn 1.
- **Healer's Hawk:** a 1/1 flying lifelink creature that gains life every
  combat. It is also a cheap flyer for Archangel.
- **Lunarch Veteran:** gains 1 life whenever another creature enters under
  your control, and can be recast from the graveyard as a flying Spirit.
- **Vampire Nighthawk:** a flying, deathtouch, lifelink body. It blocks and
  races at once and is the best defender.

### Payoffs

- **Ajani's Pridemate:** grows with every life gain. It is a threat on turn
  2 that gets larger quickly.
- **Marauding Blight-Priest:** each opponent loses 1 life whenever you gain
  life, so a single gain is also damage.
- **Righteous Valkyrie:** a flyer that gains life when an Angel or Cleric
  enters, and gives +2/+2 to your creatures at 27 or more life.
- **Archangel of Thune:** a flying lifelinker that puts a counter on each
  creature you control whenever you gain life. It wins the game on its own.
- **Vito, Thorn of the Dusk Rose:** makes the opponent lose life equal to
  your gain, and pays {3}{B}{B} to give your team lifelink for a turn.
- **Sanguine Bond:** a drain engine that makes the opponent lose life equal
  to the life you gain.
- **Heliod, Sun-Crowned:** puts a counter on a creature or enchantment you
  control on each life gain, and gives another creature lifelink. It is not
  a creature unless devotion to white is five or more.

### Removal and Support

- **Kaya's Guile:** choose two of: the opponent sacrifices a creature, exile
  their graveyards, make a Spirit, or gain 4 life. Use it as removal and a
  life gain in one card.
- **Sorin, Vengeful Bloodlord:** gives your creatures and planeswalkers
  lifelink on your turn, pings the opponent, and returns a creature from
  the graveyard with its minus ability.

### Win Conditions

- **Felidar Sovereign:** a 4/6 lifelinker that wins the game on your upkeep
  if you have 40 or more life. It is also a sturdy blocker.

### Mana

- **Godless Shrine, Concealed Courtyard, Scoured Barrens:** dual lands that
  make both colors. Scoured Barrens gains 1 life when it enters, so it
  triggers every payoff.
- **Plains and Swamp:** the basic lands. White is the main color.

### Mulligan

Keep hands with 2 to 4 lands and a cheap life gain creature such as Soul
Warden or Healer's Hawk, ideally with a payoff. Mulligan hands full of
payoffs but no way to gain life.

### Turn Guide

1. Turn 1: Soul Warden, Healer's Hawk, or Lunarch Veteran.
2. Turn 2: Ajani's Pridemate, Marauding Blight-Priest, or a second
   life gain creature.
3. Turn 3: Vampire Nighthawk, Righteous Valkyrie, or Heliod.
4. Turn 4: Archangel of Thune, Sorin, Vito, or Sanguine Bond.
5. Turns 5 to 7: attack with lifelink creatures, drain with Vito and Sanguine
   Bond, and finish with Archangel or Felidar Sovereign.

### Matchups/Cautions

- **Good against:** Burn, Prowess, and Group slug, since you outgain their
  damage.
- **Struggles against:** Infect (poison ignores life) and Mill.
- **Caution:** Heliod is not a creature without five devotion to white, so
  it is a support piece, not a blocker. Keep a removal spell or Nighthawk
  back for a faster opponent's best creature.
