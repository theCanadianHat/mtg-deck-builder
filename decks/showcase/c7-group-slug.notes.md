# C7 Group Slug: Notes

Part of the [archetype showcase](ARCHETYPES.md). Shared rules and power band
live there.

## Constraints

- Rakdos (BR). Every card hurts both players or wins the race.
- No exclusions.

## Primer

### Game Plan

Punish everyone each turn: damage for drawing, for tapping lands, for
playing lands. You built for the pain and they did not, so they die first.
Wins around turns 6 to 8.

### Key Synergies

- Wheel of Fortune and Reforge the Soul give both players seven cards, so
  Spiteful Visions, Underworld Dreams, and Megrim hit hard right after.
- Sulfuric Vortex and Rampaging Ferocidon stop lifegain, so the race cannot
  be undone by healing.
- Ankh of Mishra, Zo-Zu, and Manabarbs punish land drops and mana use; stack
  them only when you are ahead on life.

### Symmetric Damage

- **Sulfuric Vortex:** each player takes 2 damage in their own upkeep, and
  nobody can gain life. A fast clock once you are ahead.
- **Ankh of Mishra:** deals 2 damage to a land's controller each time a land
  enters, which punishes both players' land drops.
- **Zo-Zu the Punisher:** a creature version of Ankh of Mishra that also
  deals damage whenever a land enters.
- **Manabarbs:** deals 1 damage to a player whenever they tap a land for
  mana; the cost scales with how many spells they cast.
- **Spiteful Visions:** each player draws an extra card in their draw step
  and takes 1 damage for each card drawn.

### Opponent-Only Punishers

- **Underworld Dreams:** deals 1 damage to an opponent each time they draw a
  card; one-sided, and strong with Wheel of Fortune.
- **Megrim:** deals 2 damage to an opponent whenever they discard a card.
  Wheel effects trigger it for each card in their hand.
- **Painful Quandary:** whenever an opponent casts a spell, they must lose 5
  life or discard a card; it taxes every spell they play.

### Wheels

- **Wheel of Fortune:** each player discards their hand and draws seven;
  cast it after punishers are out for maximum damage.
- **Reforge the Soul:** the same wheel effect, and can be cast through its
  miracle cost when drawn as the first card of a turn.

### Burn

- **Flame Rift:** 4 damage to each player; finish when you are ahead.
- **Rakdos Charm:** a flexible instant; it can exile a graveyard, destroy
  an artifact, or make each creature deal 1 damage to its
  controller, which hits opponents with wide boards.
- **Price of Progress:** damages each player based on their nonbasic lands;
  cast it when they are on nonbasics and you are not.

### Creatures

- **Rampaging Ferocidon:** a 3/3 menace body that stops lifegain and deals 1
  damage to the controller of any other creature that enters.

### Mana

- **Blood Crypt (4):** a dual land that fixes both colors.
- **Bloodstained Mire (4):** fetches Blood Crypt, Mountain, or Swamp.
- **Sulfurous Springs (2):** a painland that taps for colored mana at the
  cost of 1 damage to you.
- **Mountain (7) and Swamp (5):** the basic lands.

### Mulligan

Keep hands with 2 to 4 lands and a one- or two-mana punisher or a burn
spell. Mulligan hands with only wheels and no early game against aggro.

### Turn Guide

1. Turn 1: play a land; a cheap punisher or Sulfuric Vortex if you are on
   the play against a slow deck.
2. Turn 2: Sulfuric Vortex, Ankh of Mishra, or Rampaging Ferocidon.
3. Turn 3: Manabarbs, Zo-Zu, Underworld Dreams, or Megrim.
4. Turn 4: Spiteful Visions or Painful Quandary.
5. Turns 5-6: Wheel of Fortune or Reforge the Soul once the punishers are
   in play.
6. Turns 7-8: Flame Rift, Price of Progress, or Rakdos Charm to finish.

### Matchups/Cautions

- **Good against:** slow control and Lifegain (Vortex and Ferocidon stop
  gains).
- **Struggles against:** fast aggro, which races you, and Turbo fog's
  taxes.
- **Caution:** check life totals before each symmetric card; only cast it
  if you win the race.
