# K5 Dredge: Notes

Part of the [archetype showcase](ARCHETYPES.md). Shared rules and power band
live there.

## Constraints

- Rakdos (BR) with blue graveyard cards that never need casting. Every card
  dredges, discards, or works from the graveyard.
- No exclusions.

## Primer

### Game Plan

Replace draws with dredge, milling several cards each time, and win from the
graveyard: creatures return for free, and Creeping Chill drains when milled.
Wins around turns 4 to 5.

### Key Synergies

- Dredge replaces your draw with returning the dredger and milling cards, so
  the graveyard fills and the free creatures come back.
- Narcomoeba and Creeping Chill trigger when milled, so dredging is also
  your damage.
- Prized Amalgam returns whenever a creature enters from your graveyard.
- Your hand matters very little; your graveyard is your hand.

### Dredgers

- **Stinkweed Imp:** a flying blocker with dredge 5 that can also destroy a
  creature it damages in combat.
- **Golgari Thug:** a cheap creature with dredge 4 that puts a creature card
  from the graveyard on top of your library when it dies.
- **Golgari Grave-Troll:** dredge 6 and a large body that grows with the
  creatures in your graveyard.
- **Dakmor Salvage:** a land with dredge 2 that enters tapped and taps for
  black; it can come back from the graveyard in place of a draw when you need a land
  drop.

### Discard Outlets

- **Faithless Looting:** draws two and discards two, with flashback; the
  first card that puts a dredger in the graveyard.
- **Cathartic Reunion:** discards two to draw three; fills the graveyard in
  one go.
- **Conflagrate:** deals X damage divided as you choose; it also has
  flashback by discarding cards, a finisher or a sweeper.
- **Darkblast:** -1/-1 until end of turn for {B}, so it kills an X/1,
  with dredge 3.

### Free Value

- **Narcomoeba:** enters the battlefield from the library when milled for
  free; a flying body.
- **Creeping Chill:** drains the opponent for 3 and gains you 3 when milled
  from the library.
- **Bloodghast:** a recurring attacker that returns whenever you play a
  land; a way to use your graveyard each turn.
- **Prized Amalgam:** returns from the graveyard to the battlefield when a
  creature enters from your graveyard.
- **Ichorid:** returns on its own upkeep by exiling a black creature card
  from your graveyard, attacks, then dies.

### Reanimation

- **Dread Return:** returns the best creature from your graveyard to the
  battlefield; also has a flashback option by sacrificing creatures.

### Mana

- **Blood Crypt (4):** a dual land that fixes both colors.
- **Bloodstained Mire (4):** a fetch that finds Blood Crypt, Swamp, or
  Mountain.
- **Mountain (5) and Swamp (4):** basic lands.

### Mulligan

Keep hands with 1 to 3 lands (a fetch is excellent), a discard outlet or a
dredger, and a graveyard payoff. Mulligan hands with only expensive cards or
no lands.

### Turn Guide

1. Turn 1: land and Faithless Looting, discarding a dredger.
2. Turn 2: Cathartic Reunion or another outlet; fill the graveyard.
3. Turn 3: start dredging in place of drawing; recur Bloodghast and Ichorid
   with land drops.
4. Turns 4-5: attack with the recurring creatures; Creeping Chill, Dread
   Return, and Conflagrate close the game.

### Matchups/Cautions

- **Good against:** fair decks with no graveyard hate.
- **Struggles against:** graveyard exile, including Scavenging Ooze in Rock
  and Toolbox.
- **Caution:** keep one draw step per turn for a land drop so Bloodghast
  returns; do not dredge your whole library away.
