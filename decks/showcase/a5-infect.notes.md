# A5 Infect: Notes

Part of the [archetype showcase](ARCHETYPES.md). Shared rules and power band
live there.

## Goals

- Infect creatures deal damage as poison counters; 10 poison wins.
- One pump spell on an unblocked creature can win on turn 3 or 4.

## Constraints

- Simic (UG). Every creature has infect; every spell pumps or protects.
- No exclusions.

## Primer

### Game Plan

Attack with one infect creature and hold pump plus protection. If the
opponent blocks or tries to kill it, respond. Since you need 10 poison
instead of 20 life, one pump spell on an unblocked creature can win on turn
3 or 4. Ignore their life total; only poison counts.

### Key Synergies

- **Infect and pump:** infect damage is poison counters, so +2/+2 is 2
  more poison and +4/+4 is 4 more.
- **Exalted:** Noble Hierarch gives a lone attacker +1/+1.
- **Protect the attacker:** hexproof spells answer removal in response.
- **Evasion:** Blighted Agent, Inkmoth Nexus, Distortion Strike, and Rancor
  make the attacker hard to block.

### Infect Threats

- **Glistener Elf:** a 1/1 infect creature for {G}. Cast it on turn 1 to
  start the clock.
- **Blighted Agent:** a 1/1 infect creature that cannot be blocked. The
  best target for pump spells.
- **Plague Stinger:** a 1/1 flying infect creature. It costs {1}{B}, and
  this deck has no black mana source, so it is likely uncastable as
  built.
- **Inkmoth Nexus:** a land that becomes a 1/1 flying infect artifact
  creature for {1} until end of turn. It dodges sorcery-speed removal and
  sweepers.

### Pump

- **Mutagenic Growth:** +2/+2 until end of turn, free if you pay 2 life.
  Pump after blockers, or in response to burn.
- **Groundswell:** +2/+2 until end of turn, or +4/+4 if you played a land
  this turn (landfall). Play a land first.
- **Might of Old Krosa:** +2/+2 until end of turn, or +4/+4 if cast
  during your main phase. Cast it precombat when the creature is safe.
- **Become Immense:** +6/+6 until end of turn, and it has delve, so it is
  cheap late in the game. The biggest finisher in the deck.

### Protection and Evasion

- **Vines of Vastwood:** gives hexproof to a creature. If kicked with {G},
  it also gets +4/+4. Use it in response to removal.
- **Blossoming Defense:** +2/+2 and hexproof until end of turn. A cheap
  protection spell that is also a pump.
- **Distortion Strike:** a sorcery that gives +1/+0 and makes a creature
  unblockable this turn, then rebounds. Use it to push a pump through.
- **Rancor:** an Aura that gives +2/+0 and trample. It returns to your hand
  if put into the graveyard, so it is resilient to removal.
- **Spell Pierce:** counters a noncreature spell unless its controller
  pays {2}. Stops removal or sweepers before you attack.

### Mana

- **Noble Hierarch:** a mana creature that makes colored mana and has
  exalted. It accelerates a turn-1 play and pumps a lone attacker.
- **Breeding Pool:** a shock land for green and blue. Pay 2 life to
  enter untapped.
- **Misty Rainforest:** fetches Breeding Pool, Forest, or Island.
- **Pendelhaven:** a land that taps for {G}. Its ability gives a 1/1
  creature +1/+2, so it can pump an infect creature.
- **Forest (3) and Island (3):** basic lands for the two colors.

### Mulligan

Keep hands with 2 lands (or a land and Noble Hierarch), an infect
creature, and a pump spell. Mulligan hands with no creature or only
spells.

### Turn Guide

1. Turn 1: Glistener Elf or Noble Hierarch.
2. Turn 2: Blighted Agent or a second creature. Attack with the best
   infect creature and hold up pump and protection.
3. Turn 3: attack with pump spells, such as Groundswell after a land, or
   Might of Old Krosa precombat. Add Rancor to a creature.
4. Turn 4: finish with Become Immense, or Vines of Vastwood to protect the
   last attack. Inkmoth Nexus can be animated to finish.

### Matchups and Cautions

- **Good against:** slow decks and lifegain (life does not stop poison).
- **Struggles against:** cheap removal held up at instant speed (Flash
  tempo, Draw-go).
- Play around removal by keeping Vines of Vastwood or Blossoming Defense
  open before committing a pump spell.
