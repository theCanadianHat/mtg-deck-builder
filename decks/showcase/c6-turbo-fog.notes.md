# C6 Turbo Fog: Notes

Part of the [archetype showcase](ARCHETYPES.md). Shared rules and power band
live there.

## Constraints

- Azorius (WU). Every card taxes attacks, prevents damage, draws, or wins.
- No exclusions.

## Primer

### Game Plan

Make attacking too costly with tax enchantments, prevent the damage that
gets through with Fogs, and win slowly with Dream Trawler or Celestial
Colonnade. Stabilizes around turn 4 and wins around turn 12 or later.

### Key Synergies

- Propaganda, Ghostly Prison, and Sphere of Safety stack: each tax is paid
  per attacker, so several together make attacks very costly.
- Fogs and Fog Bank cover the turns when an opponent pays the taxes anyway.
- Dream Trawler draws cards when you attack and cast spells, so card draw
  and the win condition feed each other.

### Attack Taxes

- **Propaganda:** creatures cannot attack you unless their controller pays
  {2} for each one. Cast it on turn 3.
- **Ghostly Prison:** the same {2}-per-attacker tax; a second copy stacks
  the cost.
- **Sphere of Safety:** attackers cost {X} each, where X is the number of
  enchantments you control; it grows with Propaganda and Prison.

### Fogs and Blockers

- **Holy Day:** for {W}, prevents all combat damage this turn. Cast it in
  the declare-blockers step after attackers are committed.
- **Dawn Charm:** a flexible instant: prevent all combat damage, regenerate a
  creature, or counter a spell that targets you (a hard counter,
  so it stops burn aimed at your face).
- **Fog Bank:** a flying defender that prevents combat damage dealt to and
  by it; a wall that holds off one attacker every turn.

### Card Draw and Dig

- **Ponder:** looks at the top three cards, then rearranges them or
  shuffles, and draws; find taxes and land early.
- **Hieroglyphic Illumination:** draws two at instant speed and can be
  cycled early; cast it at the end of their turn.
- **Sphinx's Revelation:** an X spell that draws X cards and gains X life;
  cast it late for a huge refill and a life buffer.

### Control

- **Counterspell:** stops a key spell such as removal on your win condition
  or a spell that goes around your taxes.
- **Supreme Verdict:** a sorcery that destroys all creatures and cannot be
  countered; use it when taxes fall behind or they flood the board.

### Win Conditions

- **Dream Trawler:** a big flying lifelinker that draws when it attacks, and
  can discard a card to gain hexproof, which also taps it. Use that only
  against removal on your turn after it attacks, or on their turn.
- **Celestial Colonnade:** a land that becomes a 4/4 flying vigilance
  creature for a turn; a win condition that dodges sorcery-speed removal.

### Mana

- **Hallowed Fountain (4) and Glacial Fortress (3):** dual lands that
  support both colors.
- **Mystic Gate (2):** a hybrid land that fixes white and blue.
- **Celestial Colonnade (2):** a dual land and finisher.
- **Island (8) and Plains (6):** the basic lands.

### Mulligan

Keep hands with 3 to 5 lands, an early tax (Propaganda or Prison), and some
form of interaction or card draw. Mulligan hands with no lands or with only
the expensive finishers.

### Turn Guide

1. Turns 1-2: play lands and Ponder to find the taxes.
2. Turn 3: Propaganda or Ghostly Prison.
3. Turn 4: a second tax or Fog Bank; hold Counterspell or a Fog.
4. Turns 5-6: Sphere of Safety, Supreme Verdict if needed, and card draw.
5. Turn 7 and later: Dream Trawler, Sphinx's Revelation, or Colonnade;
   begin attacking when you are safe.

### Matchups/Cautions

- **Good against:** creature aggro of every kind.
- **Struggles against:** Burn, Mill, Storm, and any deck that does not
  rely on attacking.
- **Caution:** fog in the declare-blockers step so they commit first, and
  keep mana for a Fog when your taxes are not yet down.
