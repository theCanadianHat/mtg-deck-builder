# C3 Superfriends: Notes

Part of the [archetype showcase](ARCHETYPES.md). Shared rules and power band
live there.

## Goals

- Planeswalkers backed by sweepers and counters.
- Keep the board clear, and the walkers generate value every turn until one
  ultimates.
- Stabilizes around turn 5 and wins around turns 9 to 11.

## Constraints

- Esper (WUB). Every card is a walker or protects them.
- No exclusions.

## Primer

### Game Plan

Deploy a walker only when it can protect itself (it makes a blocker or
removes a threat) or the board is empty. Use sweepers, removal, and
counters to keep the board clear, spread pressure with two walkers, and
let one ultimate. Stabilizes around turn 5 and wins around turns 9 to 11.

### Key Synergies

- **Oath of Teferi:** lets you activate each planeswalker's loyalty
  ability twice per turn, so walkers tick up or ultimate quickly.
- **Protection:** sweepers, removal, and counters keep attackers off your
  walkers.
- **Two walkers:** force the opponent to split attacks.
- **Teferi and Narset:** Teferi, Time Raveler restricts the opponent to
  sorcery speed; Narset limits their card draw.

### Planeswalkers

- **Teferi, Time Raveler:** opponents can only cast spells when they could
  cast a sorcery. +1 lets you cast sorceries at instant speed, and -3
  bounces an artifact, creature, or enchantment and draws a card.
- **Narset, Parter of Veils:** opponents cannot draw more than one card
  each turn. -2 digs for a noncreature, nonland card. Strong against
  card-draw decks.
- **Kaya, Orzhov Usurper:** one copy. +1 exiles up to two cards from one graveyard
  (gaining 2 life if a creature was exiled), and -1 exiles a nonland
  permanent with mana value 1 or less. Graveyard hate that kills tokens
  and one-drops.
- **Jace, the Mind Sculptor:** +2 fateseals, 0 brainstorms, -1 bounces a
  creature. Use the -1 to protect itself and the brainstorm for card
  advantage.
- **The Wandering Emperor:** has flash and can activate at instant speed
  the turn it enters. -2 exiles a tapped creature, and she makes Samurai.
- **Sorin, Lord of Innistrad:** +1 makes a 1/1 lifelink Vampire, a
  blocker for your walkers. Its emblem pumps creatures.
- **Teferi, Hero of Dominaria:** +1 draws a card and untaps two lands at the
  end of turn. -3 puts a nonland permanent into its owner's library.
- **Elspeth, Sun's Champion:** +1 makes three 1/1 Soldiers, and -3 destroys
  all creatures with power 4 or greater. The best defensive walker.
- **Ugin, the Spirit Dragon:** +2 deals 3 damage to any target, and -X
  exiles each colored permanent with mana value X or less. A top-end
  board reset.

### Walker Support

- **Oath of Teferi:** a legendary enchantment. When it enters, exile
  another permanent you control; it returns at the next end step, so blink
  a planeswalker to reset its loyalty. Lets each walker
  activate twice per turn.

### Interaction

- **Path to Exile:** exiles a creature, and its controller searches for a
  basic land. Use it on an attacker threatening a walker.
- **Thoughtseize:** look at their hand and discard a nonland card, losing 2
  life. Cast it early to take a sweeper or key threat.
- **Despark:** exiles a permanent with mana value 4 or greater. An answer to
  a big threat or planeswalker.
- **Dovin's Veto:** counters a noncreature spell, and cannot be countered.
  Use it on removal or a planeswalker.
- **Counterspell:** counters a target spell for {U}{U}. Hold it up when
  you tap out less.
- **Esper Charm:** choose one: destroy an enchantment, draw two cards, or a
  player discards two cards. A flexible instant.
- **Detention Sphere:** exiles a nonland permanent and all permanents with
  the same name. An answer to a planeswalker or a token army.
- **Supreme Verdict:** destroys all creatures, and cannot be countered. The
  sweeper that resets the board.

### Mana

- **Hallowed Fountain, Watery Grave, and Godless Shrine:** shock lands for
  two of the three colors. Pay 2 life to enter untapped.
- **Flooded Strand and Marsh Flats:** fetch lands that find shock lands or
  basics of the right color.
- **Raffine's Tower:** a white, blue, and black land that enters tapped.
  Cycle it for {3} late.
- **Island (4), Plains (4), and Swamp (3):** basic lands for the colors.

### Mulligan

Keep hands with 3 or 4 lands (including both colors), and at least one
sweeper, removal spell, or cheap walker. Mulligan hands with only
planeswalkers and no way to protect them.

### Turn Guide

1. Turns 1 to 2: lands, and Thoughtseize if you have the mana.
2. Turn 3: Teferi, Time Raveler or Narset on an empty board, or hold
   removal.
3. Turn 4: Jace, the Mind Sculptor, The Wandering Emperor, or Supreme
   Verdict if the opponent has creatures. Oath of Teferi on a board with
   walkers.
4. Turn 5: Teferi, Hero of Dominaria or Elspeth, Sun's Champion once the
   board is safe.
5. Turns 6 to 11: protect walkers with Supreme Verdict, Detention Sphere,
   and counters, and ultimate with Oath of Teferi.

### Matchups and Cautions

- **Good against:** midrange and fair creature decks.
- **Struggles against:** fast aggro (Burn, Prowess) and flyers.
- The legend rule applies; hold duplicates as backups.
