Anatomy of a Magic Turn

5 phases · 12 steps/phases · what you can cast or activate in each
Instant speed: instants, flash permanents, activated abilities, triggers (any player with priority) Sorcery speed: only active player, own main phase, empty stack No priority given

1. Beginning Phase

3 steps

Untap

No priority
  • Active player untaps all permanents
  • Phasing happens first
  • Nothing can be cast; "beginning of untap" effects are rare

Upkeep

Instant speed
  • Instants & flash creatures
  • Activated abilities
  • "At the beginning of your upkeep" triggers (e.g. Phyrexian Arena-style, Vampiric Link)

Draw

Instant speed
  • Active player draws a card (no priority during the draw itself)
  • Then priority: instants, flash, abilities

2. Precombat Main

1 phase

Main Phase 1

Sorcery speed+ Instant
  • Land – 1 per turn (special action, doesn't use stack)
  • Creatures
  • Sorceries
  • Artifacts & Equipment (and Equip)
  • Enchantments (Auras, Sagas, Classes, Rooms)
  • Planeswalkers + one loyalty ability each
  • Battles
  • Instants, flash cards, activated abilities
  • Sorcery-speed abilities (Equip, Level up, Crew timing is instant)
  • Saga lore counter added at start of this phase
Stack must be empty and you must hold priority. Common spot to cast ramp, sweepers, and setup before attacking.

3. Combat Phase

5 steps (6 with first/double strike)

Beginning of Combat

Instant speed
  • Last chance to tap/remove potential blockers or attackers
  • Flash creatures, removal, pump

Declare Attackers

Instant speed
  • Attackers chosen & tapped; "whenever attacks" triggers
  • Defender: flash blockers, removal, fog

Declare Blockers

Instant speed
  • Combat tricks, pump, protection, removal
  • Skipped if no attackers

Combat Damage

Instant speed
  • Damage dealt simultaneously (extra First-strike step happens first if relevant)
  • After damage: instants, "deals combat damage" triggers

End of Combat

Instant speed
  • Instants, abilities, "end of combat" triggers

4. Postcombat Main

1 phase

Main Phase 2

Sorcery speed+ Instant
  • Same as Main Phase 1
  • Land drop (if not made yet)
  • Creatures, sorceries, artifacts, enchantments, planeswalkers, battles
  • Instants and abilities
Cast creatures here to hide info before combat, or after seeing combat results. Hold the land drop to bluff.

5. Ending Phase

2 steps

End Step

Instant speed
  • Instants and flash creatures (classic "end of turn" plays)
  • Activated abilities
  • "At the beginning of the end step" triggers

Cleanup

Usually no priority
  • Active player discards down to 7 cards
  • Damage wears off; "until end of turn" effects end
  • Priority only if a state trigger/trigger fires, then another cleanup step
Rules of thumb:
  • Instants, flash, and abilities can be used any time you have priority (every step except Untap and usually Cleanup).
  • Lands, creatures, sorceries, artifacts, enchantments, planeswalkers, and battles (without flash) require your own main phase with an empty stack.
  • In multiplayer (Commander), non-active players receive priority in every step too, so they can respond at instant speed on your turn.
