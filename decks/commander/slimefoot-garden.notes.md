# Slimefoot's Garden: Notes

## Goals

- Commander: Slimefoot and Squee (Jund). A gardener deck: plant seeds
  (Saproling and Plant tokens), water and grow them (+1/+1 counters and
  lords), compost the dead (graveyard recursion), and harvest (big attacks
  and sacrifice payoffs).
- Fun first. Target bracket 3 with low (bracket 2) and high swap options.

## Constraints

- Proxied, so no budget limit, but every card must earn its slot.
- Zero Game Changers in the main list.
- No infinite combos, extra turns, or mass land destruction.

## Primer

### Game Plan

A gardener deck. Plant seeds (Saproling and Plant tokens), water them
(+1/+1 counters and lords), compost the dead (graveyard recursion), and
harvest (a big trampling attack or a sacrifice drain). Slimefoot and Squee
rebuilds the board after a wipe. Wins around turns 8 to 10.

### Commander

- **Slimefoot and Squee:** creates a 1/1 Saproling whenever it enters or
  attacks. At sorcery speed, pay {1}{B}{R}{G} and sacrifice a Saproling to
  return it and up to one other creature card from your graveyard to the
  battlefield. After a wipe, any surviving or new Saproling brings back
  your commander plus your best dead creature, without commander tax.

### Seeds: Token Makers

These put the bodies on the board that everything else grows and spends.

- **Saproling Migration:** two Saprolings for {1}{G}, or four kicked for
  {4} more.
- **Sprout Swarm:** instant Saproling with convoke and buyback {3}. Tap
  your tokens to pay for it and buy it back every turn as a mana sink.
- **Jade Mage:** {2}{G} for a Saproling, repeatable. An early mana sink.
- **Yavimaya Sapherd:** a 2/2 Fungus that brings a Saproling with it.
- **Jund Battlemage:** {G} and tap for a Saproling, or {B} and tap to make
  a player lose 1 life.
- **Utopia Mycon:** gains a spore counter each upkeep and makes a Saproling
  for three. Sacrifice a Saproling to add one mana of any color, which
  fixes colors and feeds Slimefoot, the Stowaway.
- **Deathspore Thallid:** spore counters make Saprolings. Sacrifice a
  Saproling to give a creature -1/-1, which kills x/1 attackers and
  utility creatures.
- **Sporesower Thallid:** a 4/4 that puts a spore counter on every Fungus
  you control each upkeep, so all your Thallids and Thelon-pumped Fungi
  grow together.
- **Sporemound:** a Saproling on every land drop. Fetch lands and ramp
  spells make more.
- **Tendershoot Dryad:** a Saproling at the beginning of each upkeep,
  including opponents'. With ten or more permanents (ascend), your
  Saprolings get +2/+2. Kill-on-sight for opponents, so cast it when you
  can protect it.
- **Grismold, the Dreadsower:** a 3/3 trampler. Each player creates a 1/1
  Plant at your end step, and Grismold gets a +1/+1 counter whenever
  any creature token dies. Opponents get chump blockers too, so count
  them before you attack.
- **Nemata, Grove Guardian:** {2}{G} for a Saproling, and sacrifice a
  Saproling to give all Saprolings +1/+1 until end of turn. Late-game,
  sacrifice spare tokens during combat to pump the rest.
- **Verdeloth the Ancient:** kicked for X, it makes X Saprolings. Gives
  Saprolings and other Treefolk +1/+1.
- **Mycoloth:** devour 2 when it enters, then one Saproling per +1/+1
  counter each upkeep. Devour two or three tokens to start a Saproling
  engine.
- **Avenger of Zendikar:** a 0/1 Plant for each land you control, then
  +1/+1 on every Plant per land drop. One of the main finishers.
- **Phylath, World Sculptor:** a 0/1 Plant per basic land you control.
  Each land drop puts four +1/+1 counters on one Plant.
- **Nissa, Voice of Zendikar:** +1 makes a 0/1 Plant each turn. −2 puts a
  +1/+1 counter on each creature you control.

### Water and Sun: Counters and Lords

These turn 1/1 tokens into a threatening board.

- **Hardened Scales:** every time counters go on a creature you control,
  it gets one more. Bristly Bill, Ouroboroid, Phylath and Mazirek all
  scale with it.
- **Doubling Season:** doubles tokens you create and counters put on your
  permanents. The strongest single growth piece.
- **Bristly Bill, Spine Sower:** each land drop puts a +1/+1 counter on a
  creature. {3}{G}{G} doubles the counters on each of your creatures.
- **Ouroboroid:** at the beginning of combat on your turn, puts X +1/+1
  counters on each creature you control, where X is its own power (1 at
  first). Grow it first and the whole team grows faster.
- **Mazirek, Kraul Death Priest:** whenever any player sacrifices another
  permanent, put a +1/+1 counter on each creature you control. Saproling
  sacrifices, fetch lands, Clues, Goblin Bombardment and Mycoloth's devour
  all trigger it.
- **Thelon of Havenwood:** Fungus creatures get +1/+1 per spore counter on
  them. Your Thallids, Utopia Mycon and Sporemound are Fungi, but
  Saproling tokens are not. {B}{G} and exiling a Fungus card from a
  graveyard puts a spore counter on each Fungus.
- **Rampant Rejuvenator:** enters with two +1/+1 counters. When it dies,
  fetch basic lands equal to its power, which triggers every landfall
  card. Grow it before it dies.
- **Dreg Mangler:** a 3/3 haste attacker. Scavenge it from the graveyard
  later to put three +1/+1 counters on a creature.

### Gardeners: Ramp and Mana

Land drops are a resource here: every extra land triggers Bristly Bill,
Sporemound, Phylath, Avenger of Zendikar and Tireless Tracker.

- **Sol Ring:** {C}{C} from turn one.
- **Arcane Signet:** any color in your commander's identity.
- **Wall of Roots:** a 0/5 blocker that can add {G} once each turn, even
  on an opponent's turn.
- **Sylvan Caryatid:** hexproof mana of any color.
- **Ilysian Caryatid:** mana of any color, or two mana of one color once
  you control a creature with power 4 or greater.
- **Goobbue Gardener:** a 1/3 that taps for {G}.
- **Nature's Lore:** a Forest card onto the battlefield untapped.
  Ziatora's Proving Ground, Overgrown Tomb and Stomping Ground are
  Forests, so it fixes colors too.
- **Three Visits:** a second copy of Nature's Lore.
- **Farseek:** a Swamp or Mountain card (including the shocks and Ziatora's
  Proving Ground) onto the battlefield tapped.
- **Cultivate:** one basic land onto the battlefield tapped and one to
  hand.
- **Kodama's Reach:** a second copy of Cultivate.
- **Explosive Vegetation:** two basic lands onto the battlefield, for two
  landfall triggers in one spell.
- **Life and Limb:** all Forests and Saprolings are both 1/1 Saproling
  creatures and Forest lands. Saprolings tap for {G}, and Saproling pumps
  (Verdeloth the Ancient, Tendershoot Dryad, and Nemata, Grove Guardian's
  sacrifice ability) also pump your Forests.

### Compost: Recursion

Dead creatures are fuel. These turn the graveyard into new tokens or
bring the best creatures back.

- **Insidious Roots:** your creature tokens tap for mana of any color.
  Whenever creature cards leave your graveyard, create a 0/1 Plant and
  put a +1/+1 counter on each Plant. Slimefoot and Squee, Eternal
  Witness, Rot Farm Skeleton, Dreg Mangler's scavenge, and Night Soil or
  Necrogenesis exiling from your own graveyard all trigger it.
- **Night Soil:** {1} and exile two creature cards from one graveyard for
  a Saproling.
- **Necrogenesis:** {2} to exile a creature card from any graveyard for a
  Saproling. Also stops opposing reanimation.
- **Golgari Germination:** a Saproling whenever a nontoken creature you
  control dies.
- **Yedora, Grave Gardener:** when another nontoken creature you control
  dies, return it face down as a Forest land. That counts as a land
  entering, so it triggers landfall, and with Life and Limb it becomes a
  Saproling creature.
- **Rot Farm Skeleton:** a 4/1 that can't block. Pay {2}{B}{G} and mill
  four to return it from your graveyard at sorcery speed. The mill also
  stocks Night Soil and Necrogenesis.
- **Eternal Witness:** returns any card from your graveyard to hand.

### Harvest: Payoffs and Finishers

- **Overwhelming Stampede:** your creatures gain trample and get +X/+X,
  where X is the greatest power among them. The main kill with a wide
  board.
- **Return of the Wildspeaker:** non-Human creatures get +3/+3 at instant
  speed for a surprise lethal attack, or draw cards equal to the greatest
  power among them.
- **Garruk's Uprising:** your creatures have trample. Draws a card when a
  creature with power 4 or greater enters.
- **Slimefoot, the Stowaway:** whenever a Saproling you control dies, each
  opponent takes 1 damage and you gain 1 life. {4} makes a Saproling.
- **Goblin Bombardment:** sacrifice a creature to deal 1 damage to any
  target. With Slimefoot, the Stowaway, each sacrificed Saproling also
  drains each opponent for 1. Each sacrifice triggers Mazirek, too.
- **Nemata, Primeval Warden:** opposing creatures that would die are exiled
  instead, and you get a Saproling. Sacrifice two Saprolings and pay
  {1}{B} to draw a card. Note it stops opposing creatures from reaching
  graveyards, so Night Soil and Necrogenesis have less to eat.

### Card Advantage

- **Tireless Tracker:** a Clue on every land drop, and it grows when you
  sacrifice Clues. Each Clue sacrifice triggers Mazirek.
- **Phyrexian Arena:** an extra card each upkeep for 1 life.
- **Harmonize:** draw three.
- **Rishkar's Expertise:** draw cards equal to your greatest power, then
  cast a spell with mana value 5 or less for free.
- **Insidious Fungus:** sacrifice it for {2} to destroy an artifact or
  enchantment, or draw a card and put a land from hand onto the
  battlefield (a landfall trigger).

### Interaction

- **Infernal Grasp:** instant creature kill for {1}{B} and 2 life.
- **Putrefy:** destroy an artifact or creature at instant speed.
- **Assassin's Trophy:** destroy any permanent an opponent controls; they
  get a basic land.
- **Beast Within:** destroy any permanent; its controller gets a 3/3.
  Answers anything, including lands and planeswalkers.
- **Chaos Warp:** shuffles any permanent into its owner's library. Red's
  catch-all answer to indestructible threats.
- **Mold Shambler:** kicked, destroys a noncreature permanent, including
  lands. A 3/3 Fungus body either way.
- **Heroic Intervention:** your permanents gain hexproof and indestructible
  until end of turn. Hold it up against board wipes once the garden is
  big.

### Mana Base

36 lands, 15 of them basic. Six fetch lands (Verdant Catacombs,
Wooded Foothills, Bloodstained Mire, Evolving Wilds, Terramorphic Expanse,
Fabled Passage) each give two landfall triggers and trigger Mazirek. Key lands:

- **Khalni Garden:** a 0/1 Plant token when it enters, for Avenger and
  Phylath to grow.
- **Dryad Arbor:** a Forest creature. Fetchable by Nature's Lore, Three
  Visits and the green fetches.
- **Yavimaya, Cradle of Growth:** every land is a Forest. With Life and
  Limb, every land becomes a 1/1 Saproling creature that your lords pump.

### Mulligan

Keep hands with 3 or more lands (or 2 lands plus a mana creature or Sol
Ring) and at least one seed or ramp piece. Land drops matter because
Bristly Bill, Sporemound, Phylath, Avenger of Zendikar and Tireless Tracker
all trigger on landfall. Mulligan hands with only expensive payoffs.

### Turn Guide

1. **Turns 1-2:** lands and a mana creature (Wall of Roots, a Caryatid,
   Goobbue Gardener) or a two-mana land search. Hardened Scales on turn 1
   if you have it.
2. **Turns 3-4:** Slimefoot and Squee, or a seed (Saproling Migration,
   Sporemound, Grismold) plus a water card (Bristly Bill, Ouroboroid).
3. **Turns 5-7:** lords and compost (Verdeloth, Night Soil, Insidious
   Roots, Golgari Germination). Avenger of Zendikar or Phylath followed by
   land drops. Keep Heroic Intervention up if you can.
4. **Turns 8-10:** harvest. Overwhelming Stampede or Return of the
   Wildspeaker for a lethal team attack, or sacrifice the board to Goblin
   Bombardment with Slimefoot, the Stowaway.

### Cautions

- After a board wipe, return Slimefoot and Squee with any Saproling, plus
  your best creature.
- Yavimaya, Cradle of Growth plus Life and Limb makes all your lands
  creatures. A creature wipe then destroys your lands too, so hold one of
  the two back if a wipe is likely.
- Grismold gives opponents chump blockers every turn.
- Tendershoot Dryad and Avenger of Zendikar draw removal. Bait with smaller
  threats first.
- Hold Goblin Bombardment until it finishes the game or saves a creature
  from exile.

## Power options

### Low: bracket 2 swaps

| Cut | Add | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Doubling Season | Fungal Plots | Slower growth |
| Ouroboroid | Psychotrope Thallid | Slower growth |
| Tendershoot Dryad | Fungal Sprouting | Fewer free tokens |
| Goblin Bombardment | Morbid Bloom | No instant-speed reach |

### High: bracket 3 to 4 swaps

Gaea's Cradle is a Game Changer. Check the current Game Changers list before
adding more than three.

| Cut | Add | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Evolving Wilds | Gaea's Cradle | Explosive mana |
| Overwhelming Stampede | Craterhoof Behemoth | Stronger finisher |
| Phyrexian Arena | Skullclamp | Draw from dying tokens |
| Jund Battlemage | Parallel Lives | Double tokens |
| Garruk's Uprising | The Great Henge | Growth and draw engine |
