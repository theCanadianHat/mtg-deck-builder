# M8 Toolbox: Notes

Part of the [archetype showcase](ARCHETYPES.md). Shared rules and power band
live there.

## Goals

- Show the midrange lane built on many single copies of answer creatures
  and tutors that find the right one for the situation.
- Wins around turns 7 to 9.

## Constraints

- Selesnya (GW). Every creature is a specific answer or value piece.
- No exclusions.

## Primer

### Game Plan

Play mana creatures, then use tutors to find the single creature that
answers the opponent's plan. Every game plays differently. Ask "what card
beats them right now?" and fetch it. Wins around turns 7 to 9.

### Key Synergies

- **Birthing Pod chain:** Birds of Paradise (1) to a two-drop to Kitchen
  Finks (3) to Restoration Angel (4) to Thragtusk or Reveillark (5). Each
  step also triggers any enter-the-battlefield effect.
- **Chord of Calling and Fauna Shaman:** reach Elesh Norn, the top-end
  creature, without needing a Pod chain.
- **Value creatures:** Kitchen Finks, Eternal Witness, Thragtusk, and
  Reveillark replace themselves when they enter or leave, so Pod and blink
  effects are card advantage.
- **Answer creatures:** one-of Qasali Pridemage, Reclamation Sage, Phyrexian
  Revoker, and Aven Mindcensor give a toolbox for each problem.

### Tutors

- **Birthing Pod:** sacrifice a creature at sorcery speed to fetch one with
  mana value one higher. It is the engine of the deck.
- **Chord of Calling:** an instant that finds any creature, with convoke to
  help pay. Use it at the end of the opponent's turn.
- **Fauna Shaman:** discards a creature card to fetch another creature. It
  turns extra copies and useless creatures into the one you need.
- **Ranger-Captain of Eos:** fetches a creature with mana value 1 or less,
  such as Birds of Paradise or Avacyn's Pilgrim, and can be sacrificed to
  stop the opponent from casting noncreature spells for a turn.

### Mana Creatures

- **Birds of Paradise:** makes any color and flies. It starts the Pod chain.
- **Avacyn's Pilgrim:** makes white mana. It is the second mana dork, and a
  mana value 1 target for Ranger-Captain.

### Answer Creatures

- **Qasali Pridemage:** destroys an artifact or enchantment when sacrificed.
  Use it on a stax piece or equipment.
- **Reclamation Sage:** destroys an artifact or enchantment when it enters.
- **Phyrexian Revoker:** names a card, and that card's activated abilities
  cannot be used. Name a planeswalker, equipment, or a mana creature.
- **Aven Mindcensor:** a flash flyer that makes opponents' searches look at
  only the top four cards. It slows tutors and fetch lands.
- **Thalia, Guardian of Thraben:** makes noncreature spells cost 1 more and
  has first strike. It slows control and combo.
- **Knight of Autumn:** chooses between two +1/+1 counters, destroying an
  artifact or enchantment, or gaining 4 life. It is the flexible answer.
- **Path to Exile:** removes any creature at instant speed. The opponent
  gets a basic land, so use it on a real threat.

### Value Creatures

- **Kitchen Finks:** gains life and persists, so it is two bodies for Pod.
- **Eternal Witness:** returns a card from your graveyard to your hand.
- **Tireless Tracker:** draws cards from land drops over a long game.
- **Selfless Spirit:** a flyer that can be sacrificed to give your team
  indestructible, which protects the board from a sweeper.
- **Scavenging Ooze:** exiles graveyard cards to grow and gain life. It
  answers recursion decks.
- **Restoration Angel:** a flash flyer that blinks a non-Angel creature
  you control and reuses its enter-the-battlefield effect.
- **Thragtusk:** gains 5 life and leaves a 3/3 Beast when it leaves.
- **Reveillark:** a flyer that returns up to two creatures with power 2 or
  less from your graveyard when it leaves.

### Finisher

- **Elesh Norn, Grand Cenobite:** gives your creatures +2/+2 and gives the
  opponent's creatures -2/-2. It ends most creature boards and wins fast.

### Mana

- **Temple Garden:** a dual land that makes both colors.
- **Windswept Heath:** fetches Forest, Plains, or Temple Garden.
- **Horizon Canopy:** makes either color at the cost of 1 life, and can be
  sacrificed to draw late.
- **Gavony Township:** a utility land that puts a counter on each of your
  creatures for a lot of mana. It is a mana sink in a long game.
- **Forest and Plains:** the basic lands.

### Mulligan

Keep hands with 2 to 4 lands, a mana creature, and a tutor or a good
creature. A hand with Birds, a land of each color, and Birthing Pod is
excellent. Hands with only expensive creatures are slow.

### Turn Guide

1. Turn 1: Birds of Paradise or Avacyn's Pilgrim.
2. Turn 2: Fauna Shaman, Thalia, Scavenging Ooze, or Birthing Pod.
3. Turn 3: Kitchen Finks, Tireless Tracker, Knight of Autumn, or Eternal
   Witness, or Pod to the next step.
4. Turn 4: Restoration Angel, Chord of Calling, or Pod up the curve.
5. Turns 5 to 9: Pod into Thragtusk and Reveillark, then Chord or Fauna
   Shaman for Elesh Norn if the board needs it.

### Matchups/Cautions

- **Good against:** decks with one key permanent (Prison, Artifact aggro),
  since you can fetch the answer.
- **Struggles against:** fast combo and sweepers.
- **Caution:** Birthing Pod and Fauna Shaman need creatures on the board,
  so sweepers and removal on mana creatures slow the deck down. Hold
  Selfless Spirit and Restoration Angel for those turns.
