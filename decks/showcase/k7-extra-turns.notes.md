# K7 Extra Turns: Notes

Part of the [archetype showcase](ARCHETYPES.md). Shared rules and power band
live there.

## Constraints

- Mono-blue. Every card takes a turn, finds one, or replays one.
- No exclusions.

## Primer

### Game Plan

Chain extra-turn spells, drawing into the next one each turn, so the
opponent never gets to play again. Recursion replays used turn spells. Wins
around turns 8 to 10, though that may be your fifth turn in a row.

### Key Synergies

- Brainstorm can put Temporal Mastery on top so you can miracle it on the
  next turn.
- Mnemonic Wall returns a used extra-turn spell; Ghostly Flicker on Mnemonic
  Wall plus another Wall can loop it.
- Walk the Aeons has buyback by sacrificing three Islands, so it is a
  repeatable extra turn while you have Islands to spare.
- Every extra turn is another attack with no blockers untapped.

### Extra-Turn Spells

- **Time Warp:** a simple extra turn at five mana; the baseline spell for
  Mnemonic Wall to return.
- **Temporal Mastery:** an extra turn that can be cast through its
  miracle cost when drawn first in a turn; Brainstorm sets it up.
- **Walk the Aeons:** an extra turn whose buyback is sacrificing three
  Islands. Use the buyback only when you have lands to spare, because each
  loop costs three land drops.
- **Temporal Trespass:** an extra turn with delve; exile graveyard cards
  to reduce the cost.
- **Nexus of Fate:** an instant extra turn that shuffles into the library
  instead of going to the graveyard; a sticky threat.

### Recursion

- **Mnemonic Wall:** returns an instant or sorcery from your graveyard to
  hand when it enters; replay Time Warp.
- **Ghostly Flicker:** exiles and returns two of your creatures, artifacts,
  or lands; blink Mnemonic Wall for another return.
- **Fact or Fiction:** reveals five cards and splits them; at instant speed,
  find extra-turn spells and Walls.

### Card Flow

- **Brainstorm:** draws three, puts two back; set up miracle draws.
- **Ponder:** looks at the top three, rearranges, draws; dig for lands and
  extra turns.

### Interaction

- **Counterspell:** protects the extra turn from a counter or answers a key
  threat on your way.

### Win Conditions

- **Lighthouse Chronologist:** a level-up creature that eventually gives you
  extra turns at the end of other players' turns.
- **Tolarian Terror:** a large body that gets cheaper with instants and
  sorceries in the graveyard and attacks on extra turns.

### Mana

- **Island (24):** every land is an Island, enabling Walk the Aeons's buyback.

### Mulligan

Keep hands with 3 to 5 lands, a cantrip or Counterspell, and a path to
five mana. Mulligan hands without lands or with only expensive extra-turn
spells and no early plays.

### Turn Guide

1. Turns 1-2: Brainstorm or Ponder; set Temporal Mastery on top.
2. Turn 3: Counterspell mana, or Fact or Fiction on the opponent's turn.
3. Turn 4: Lighthouse Chronologist or Mnemonic Wall as needed.
4. Turn 5: Time Warp or Temporal Trespass; return it with Mnemonic Wall.
5. Turns 6-10: chain extra turns; attack with Tolarian Terror each turn.

### Matchups/Cautions

- **Good against:** slow midrange and Turbo fog.
- **Struggles against:** fast aggro and counterspells.
- **Caution:** win slowly; do not tap out into a counterspell or a lethal
  attack.
