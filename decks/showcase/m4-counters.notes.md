# M4 +1/+1 Counters: Notes

Part of the [archetype showcase](ARCHETYPES.md). Shared rules and power band
live there.

## Goals

- Show the midrange lane where creatures place, double, and move +1/+1
  counters so the board grows every turn.
- Wins around turns 5 to 7.

## Constraints

- Selesnya (GW). Every card places, doubles, or rewards counters.
- No exclusions.

## Primer

### Game Plan

Play creatures that place and grow +1/+1 counters, with doublers that add
extra. Lead with Hardened Scales on turn 1 so every later counter gets
bigger. The board grows every turn and wins around turns 5 to 7.

### Key Synergies

- **Doublers stack:** Hardened Scales adds one counter, Conclave Mentor adds
  one more, and Branching Evolution doubles the total.
- **Walking Ballista and Hangarback Walker:** each counter placed makes them
  stronger, and both are still useful after removal.
- **Ozolith:** a fourth Hardened Scales effect that also covers artifacts,
  so Walking Ballista and Hangarback Walker get one extra counter too.
- **Outlast creatures with Aspirant:** Ainok Bond-Kin and Abzan Falconer
  grow and reward counters, so every counter also gives first strike or
  flying.

### Counter Makers and Early Plays

- **Experiment One:** a one-drop that evolves as bigger creatures arrive.
- **Pelt Collector:** a one-drop that grows when a creature with greater
  power enters or dies on your side, and gains trample at three counters.
- **Luminarch Aspirant:** puts a counter on a creature at the start of each
  of your combats. It is the best turn 2 play.
- **Walking Ballista:** turns spare mana into counters, then removes them to
  ping creatures or the opponent. It doubles as removal.
- **Hangarback Walker:** grows with mana, and leaves Thopters if it dies.

### Doublers

- **Hardened Scales:** one extra counter whenever counters would be placed
  on a creature you control. Cast it on turn 1.
- **Conclave Mentor:** adds one counter to every counter placement, and
  gains you life equal to its power when it dies.
- **Branching Evolution:** doubles every counter placement. Cast it when
  you can use it right away.

### Payoffs and Support

- **Avatar of the Resolute:** a reach, trample body that enters with a
  counter for each other creature of yours with a counter.
- **Ainok Bond-Kin:** gives first strike to creatures with counters, so
  they win combat. Outlast adds a counter at sorcery speed.
- **Abzan Falconer:** gives flying to your creatures with counters, which
  is how the team wins in the air. Outlast adds a counter.
- **Ozolith, the Shattered Spire:** each time +1/+1 counters would go on an
  artifact or creature you control, one more goes on. At sorcery speed,
  {1}{G} and tap it to put a counter on an artifact or creature, which
  Ozolith turns into two. Cycle it for {2} if you don't need it.
- **Dromoka's Command:** adds a counter and fights, all at instant speed. It
  can also stop a burn spell or remove an enchantment.

### Mana

- **Temple Garden:** a dual land that makes both colors.
- **Windswept Heath:** fetches Forest, Plains, or Temple Garden.
- **Horizon Canopy:** makes either color at the cost of 1 life, and can be
  sacrificed late to draw a card.
- **Forest and Plains:** the basic lands. Green is the main color.

### Mulligan

Keep hands with 2 to 4 lands and a one- or two-drop that places counters.
Hardened Scales or Luminarch Aspirant on turns 1 to 2 makes any hand good. A
hand with no early creature is slow.

### Turn Guide

1. Turn 1: Hardened Scales, Experiment One, or Pelt Collector.
2. Turn 2: Luminarch Aspirant, or a second one-drop or Walking Ballista.
3. Turn 3: Conclave Mentor, Hangarback Walker, or Ballista for 2 with
   Scales.
4. Turn 4: Branching Evolution, Avatar of the Resolute, or Dromoka's
   Command to protect or fight. Ozolith fits on turn 2 or alongside
   another spell.
5. Turns 5 to 7: use outlast and mana sinks, and attack with first strike
   and flying creatures for lethal.

### Matchups/Cautions

- **Good against:** other creature decks (Tokens, Tribal), since your
  creatures grow bigger than theirs.
- **Struggles against:** sweepers and exile removal, which take counters
  with the creature.
- **Caution:** do not overcommit to one creature. Spread counters, and keep
  Dromoka's Command for protection.
