# M7 Graveyard Value: Notes

Part of the [archetype showcase](ARCHETYPES.md). Shared rules and power band
live there.

## Goals

- Show the midrange lane that fills its own graveyard, then uses it as a
  second hand.
- Wins around turns 6 to 8.

## Constraints

- Golgari (BG). Every card fills or uses the graveyard.
- No exclusions.

## Primer

### Game Plan

Fill your own graveyard, then use it as a second hand: return creatures,
recast spells, and grow threats that scale with it. Early turns: mill and
stay alive. Midgame: return the best creature. Wins around turns 6 to 8.

### Key Synergies

- **Mill into payoffs:** Satyr Wayfinder, Stitcher's Supplier, Grisly
  Salvage, and Golgari Grave-Troll fill the graveyard for Grim Flayer,
  Splinterfright, Tasigur, and Lord of Extinction.
- **Recursion turns mill into cards:** Eternal Witness, Gravedigger, and
  Dread Return get back what you milled.
- **Life from the Loam with Dakmor Salvage:** Loam returns lands, and Dakmor
  Salvage can dredge back each turn to loop it.
- **Dredge:** Golgari Grave-Troll and Dakmor Salvage replace draws with
  milling, which fills the graveyard faster.

### Self-Mill

- **Satyr Wayfinder:** reveals four cards and takes a land into hand, with
  the rest going to the graveyard. It is a turn 2 mill and a body.
- **Stitcher's Supplier:** mills three cards when it enters and when it
  dies. It is a cheap fuel source for any graveyard plan.
- **Grisly Salvage:** reveals five cards and takes a creature or land, with
  the rest going to the graveyard. Cast it early to fill the yard.
- **Golgari Grave-Troll:** a large dredge creature that enters with a
  counter for each creature card in the graveyard. It can also dredge for 6.
- **Dakmor Salvage:** a land that dredges for 2. Dredge it back to mill
  more instead of drawing.

### Payoffs

- **Grim Flayer:** a trampler that surveils when it hits a player, and gets
  +2/+2 with four card types in the graveyard.
- **Splinterfright:** a trampler that grows with the creature cards in your
  graveyard, and mills two at each upkeep.
- **Tasigur, the Golden Fang:** delve makes it cheap, and it can return a
  nonland card from the graveyard, chosen by your opponent, to your hand.
- **Lord of Extinction:** its power and toughness equal the cards in all
  graveyards, so it can be enormous.
- **Deathrite Shaman:** exiles lands, instants, and sorceries from
  graveyards for mana or to drain, and creatures for life. It is a cheap
  mana source early and a drain later.

### Recursion

- **Eternal Witness:** returns any card from the graveyard to your hand when
  it enters. Use it on the best removal or creature.
- **Gravedigger:** returns a creature card from the graveyard to your hand
  when it enters.
- **Dread Return:** returns a creature to the battlefield, and has
  flashback by sacrificing three creatures. Use it on Lord of Extinction or
  Tasigur.
- **Life from the Loam:** returns up to three lands from the graveyard to
  your hand. It also dredges, which keeps the graveyard full.

### Removal

- **Ghastly Demise:** destroys a nonblack creature if its toughness is
  equal to or less than the cards in your graveyard. It gets better each
  turn.

### Mana

- **Overgrown Tomb, Woodland Cemetery:** dual lands that make both colors.
- **Verdant Catacombs:** fetches a Swamp or Forest, and puts a land in the
  graveyard for Deathrite Shaman and Loam.
- **Dakmor Salvage:** see Self-Mill. It also taps for black.
- **Swamp and Forest:** the basic lands.

### Mulligan

Keep hands with 2 to 4 lands and a self-mill spell or creature, plus a
payoff or recursion. Hands with only expensive payoffs and no way to fill the
graveyard are risky.

### Turn Guide

1. Turn 1: Deathrite Shaman, Stitcher's Supplier, or a land.
2. Turn 2: Satyr Wayfinder, Grisly Salvage, or Grim Flayer.
3. Turn 3: Eternal Witness, Gravedigger, Splinterfright, or Ghastly Demise.
4. Turn 4: Golgari Grave-Troll, Tasigur (cheap with delve), or Dread
   Return on the best creature.
5. Turns 5 to 8: return your best creatures, loop Life from the Loam with
   Dakmor Salvage, and win with Lord of Extinction and Tasigur.

### Matchups/Cautions

- **Good against:** one-for-one removal decks (Rock, Draw-go), since every
  removal spell leaves a card behind for you.
- **Struggles against:** graveyard exile and fast aggro.
- **Caution:** Deathrite Shaman and Tasigur's delve exile cards, so keep key
  creatures in the graveyard for Dread Return and Witness.
