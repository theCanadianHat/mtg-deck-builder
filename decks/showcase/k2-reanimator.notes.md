# K2 Reanimator: Notes

Part of the [archetype showcase](ARCHETYPES.md). Shared rules and power band
live there.

## Goals

- Show the combo lane: put a giant creature in the graveyard, then bring it
  back on turn 2 or 3.
- Wins around turns 4 to 6 once a fatty is back.

## Constraints

- Dimir (UB). Every card is a target, a way to bin a target, a way to return
  it, or cheap protection for the combo.
- No exclusions.

## Primer

### Game Plan

Put a giant creature in the graveyard, then bring it back on turn 2 or 3.
Step 1, bin a target with Unmarked Grave, Buried Alive, or a discard from
Careful Study. Step 2, return it with Animate Dead, Necromancy, or Persist.
Wins around turns 4 to 6 once a fatty is back.

### Key Synergies

- Careful Study, Thought Scour, and Consider fill the graveyard while they
  dig toward a reanimation spell.
- Unmarked Grave and Persist only work with nonlegendary targets (Archon of
  Cruelty, Grave Titan). Griselbrand needs Buried Alive or a discard.
- Thoughtseize and Spell Pierce protect the turn the combo goes off.

### Reanimation Targets

- **Archon of Cruelty:** a flying finisher; each time it enters or attacks,
  the opponent sacrifices a creature or planeswalker, discards, and loses 3
  life while you draw and gain 3.
- **Griselbrand:** a flying lifelinker that can draw seven cards for 7
  life; legendary, so only Buried Alive or a discard can bin it for
  Animate Dead or Necromancy.
- **Grave Titan:** a deathtouch creature that makes Zombies when it enters
  or attacks; the backup plan since you can cast it for six mana.

### Binners

- **Unmarked Grave:** searches for a nonlegendary card and puts it in the
  graveyard; fetches Archon or Grave Titan.
- **Buried Alive:** puts up to three creature cards from your library into
  the graveyard; the only way to bin Griselbrand from the deck.
- **Careful Study:** draws two and discards two; discard a target and find
  a returner.
- **Stitcher's Supplier:** a cheap creature that mills three cards when it
  enters or dies.
- **Thought Scour:** mills two and draws a card; a cheap way to find
  targets.

### Returners

- **Animate Dead:** returns a creature from any graveyard to the
  battlefield with -1/-0; it can steal one from the opponent's.
- **Necromancy:** returns a creature from any graveyard; can be cast
  at instant speed, so ambush the end of their turn.
- **Persist:** returns a nonlegendary creature from your graveyard with a
  -1/-1 counter; a cheap, resilient option, with no legendary targets.

### Protection and Dig

- **Thoughtseize:** shows the opponent's hand and takes a key removal or
  counter before you go.
- **Spell Pierce:** counters a noncreature spell unless they pay {2};
  protects your reanimation spell.
- **Consider:** surveils 1 and draws a card; a cheap graveyard filler.

### Mana

- **Watery Grave (4), Darkslick Shores (2), Drowned Catacomb (2):** dual
  lands for UB.
- **Polluted Delta (4):** a fetch that thins the deck and finds a dual.
- **Swamp (4) and Island (4):** basic lands.

### Mulligan

Keep hands with 2 to 4 lands and a binner, a returner, or both. A hand with
a target and a returner is ideal. Mulligan hands that are all fatties
without enablers.

### Turn Guide

1. Turn 1: land and Consider or Thought Scour; Thoughtseize if the
   opponent has a counterspell or removal.
2. Turn 2: Careful Study, Unmarked Grave, or Stitcher's Supplier; bin a
   target.
3. Turn 2-3: Animate Dead, Necromancy, or Persist on the target. Hold Spell
   Pierce when you can.
4. Turns 4-6: attack with the fatty, or cast Grave Titan if answers
   appear.

### Matchups/Cautions

- **Good against:** midrange and control without graveyard hate.
- **Struggles against:** graveyard exile and counterspells.
- **Caution:** do not discard Griselbrand without a returner available; and
  watch for the opponent stealing a target with their own reanimation.
