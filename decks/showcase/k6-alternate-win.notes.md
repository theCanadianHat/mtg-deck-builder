# K6 Alternate Win Conditions: Notes

Part of the [archetype showcase](ARCHETYPES.md). Shared rules and power band
live there.

## Constraints

- Dimir (UB). Every card wins with an empty library, empties it, or protects
  the combo. Felidar Sovereign in Lifegain is another alternate win.
- No exclusions.

## Primer

### Game Plan

Win with "you win the game" cards that trigger when your library is empty.
Empty your own library on purpose, then cast Thassa's Oracle or draw with Lab
Maniac or Jace out. Wins around turns 4 to 6.

### Key Synergies

- Cast Thassa's Oracle; with its trigger on the stack, cast Demonic
  Consultation naming a card not in your deck. It exiles your library and
  the Oracle trigger wins.
- Glimpse the Unthinkable and Thought Scour thin the library so the
  combo has less to exile.
- Counterspell and Spell Pierce protect the combo turn.

### Win Cards

- **Thassa's Oracle:** wins when its enters trigger resolves with an empty
  or small enough library.
- **Laboratory Maniac:** if you would draw from an empty library, you win
  instead; pairs with a mill of your own library.
- **Jace, Wielder of Mysteries:** a planeswalker that wins the game if you
  would draw from an empty library; also draws cards with its plus
  ability.

### Library Emptiers

- **Demonic Consultation:** names a card not in your deck and exiles your
  library; the key combo piece with Oracle.
- **Tainted Pact:** exiles cards from the top of your library one at a time
  until you stop or exile a repeat name. In this deck it mainly digs and
  thins, because copies of cards can stop it early.
- **Glimpse the Unthinkable:** mills ten cards; aim it at yourself.
- **Thought Scour:** mills two and draws a card; thins the library and digs.

### Dig

- **Brainstorm:** draws three and puts two back; digs for the combo.
- **Ponder:** looks at the top three cards and rearranges them, then draws.
- **Preordain:** scries 2, then draws; digs cheaply.

### Protection

- **Counterspell:** stops removal or a counter on the combo turn.
- **Spell Pierce:** counters a noncreature spell unless they pay {2};
  protects Consultation or Jace.
- **Thoughtseize:** takes a counterspell or removal from their hand before
  you go.

### Mana

- **Watery Grave (4), Darkslick Shores (2):** dual lands.
- **Polluted Delta (4):** a fetch that thins the library by one card.
- **Island (6) and Swamp (2):** basic lands.

### Mulligan

Keep hands with 2 to 4 lands, a win card or digging to one, and either
Consultation or protection. Mulligan hands without lands or with only
combo pieces and no way to cast them.

### Turn Guide

1. Turn 1: land and a dig spell such as Brainstorm, Ponder, or Preordain.
2. Turn 2: Thoughtseize to see their hand, or more dig.
3. Turn 3: assemble the pieces: Oracle, Consultation or Glimpse, and
   Counterspell mana.
4. Turns 4-6: go off with Oracle plus Consultation, or Lab Maniac plus an
   emptier, with protection up.

### Matchups/Cautions

- **Good against:** slow decks without counterspells.
- **Struggles against:** counterspells, discard, and Hatebears' Thalia.
- **Caution:** do not exile your library before the win card is on the
  battlefield or on the stack; protect the combo turn.
