# M2 Aristocrats: Notes

Part of the [archetype showcase](ARCHETYPES.md). Shared rules and power band
live there.

## Goals

- Show the midrange lane that makes cheap creatures, sacrifices them for
  value, and drains the opponent each time something dies.
- Wins around turns 6 to 7.

## Constraints

- Orzhov (WB). Every card is fodder, an outlet, or a death payoff.
- No exclusions.

## Primer

### Game Plan

Make cheap creatures, sacrifice them for value, and drain the opponent every
time one dies. Removal and sweepers feed your engine instead of stopping it.
Get a payoff and a sacrifice outlet down, then sacrifice in response to
removal or combat damage. Wins around turns 6 to 7.

### Key Synergies

- **Outlet plus payoff:** each sacrifice triggers every Blood Artist,
  Cruel Celebrant, and Zulaport Cutthroat, so a few creatures drain a lot.
- **Teysa Karlov:** death triggers happen twice, so every payoff doubles.
- **Recursive fodder:** Bloodghast, Kitchen Finks, Doomed Traveler, Carrier
  Thrall, and Lingering Souls leave something behind each time they die.
- **Bloodghast with land drops:** every land that enters returns it from
  the graveyard for free value.

### Sacrifice Outlets

- **Viscera Seer:** sacrifices a creature for free to scry 1. It lets you
  sacrifice in response to removal or blockers.
- **Yawgmoth, Thran Physician:** pay 1 life and sacrifice another creature
  to draw a card and put a -1/-1 counter on a creature. It has protection
  from Humans, and BB with a discard proliferates.
- **Village Rites:** an instant that sacrifices a creature to draw two
  cards. Cast it in response to removal.

### Death Payoffs

- **Blood Artist:** whenever any creature dies, a target player loses 1 life
  and you gain 1.
- **Cruel Celebrant:** whenever it or another creature or planeswalker you
  control dies, each opponent loses 1 life and you gain 1.
- **Zulaport Cutthroat:** whenever it or another creature you control dies,
  each opponent loses 1 life and you gain 1.
- **Teysa Karlov:** doubles death triggers and gives your creature tokens
  vigilance and lifelink.

### Fodder

- **Doomed Traveler:** when it dies, you get a 1/1 flying Spirit, so one
  body is two sacrifices.
- **Carrier Thrall:** when it dies, you get a 1/1 Eldrazi Scion that can be
  sacrificed for mana or as more fodder.
- **Bloodghast:** returns to the battlefield whenever a land enters under
  your control. It cannot block, so it is only fodder and an attacker.
- **Kitchen Finks:** gains 2 life and returns once with persist, giving two
  sacrifices and two life gains.
- **Lingering Souls:** two 1/1 flying Spirits, and two more with flashback.
  It is four bodies from one card.

### Mana

- **Godless Shrine, Concealed Courtyard, Caves of Koilos, Scoured Barrens:**
  dual lands that make both colors. Scoured Barrens gains 1 life when it
  enters.
- **Swamp and Plains:** the basic lands. Black is the main color.

### Mulligan

Keep hands with 2 to 4 lands, a cheap creature, and either an outlet or a
payoff. A hand with only payoffs and no bodies is weak, and a hand with only
bodies and no outlet is slow.

### Turn Guide

1. Turn 1: Viscera Seer, Doomed Traveler, or Carrier Thrall.
2. Turn 2: Bloodghast or a payoff such as Blood Artist or Cruel Celebrant.
3. Turn 3: another payoff, Kitchen Finks, or Village Rites with fodder.
4. Turn 4: Yawgmoth or Teysa Karlov, or Lingering Souls and a payoff.
5. Turns 5 to 7: sacrifice creatures with an outlet and a payoff on the
   board, aiming to drain the opponent out.

### Matchups/Cautions

- **Good against:** removal-heavy decks and sweepers (Tap-out control),
  since every removal spell feeds the engine.
- **Struggles against:** graveyard hate and fast combo. Cheap spot removal
  on a payoff or Yawgmoth also slows the deck down.
- **Caution:** do not sacrifice your only payoff for value. Keep an outlet
  and a payoff on the board together.
