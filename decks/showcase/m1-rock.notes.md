# M1 Rock: Notes

Part of the [archetype showcase](ARCHETYPES.md). Shared rules and power band
live there.

## Goals

- Show the midrange lane: trade one-for-one, then win with better cards.
- Stabilizes by turn 4 and wins around turns 7 to 9.

## Constraints

- Golgari (BG). Every card is discard, removal, a card-advantage engine, or
  an efficient threat.
- No exclusions.

## Primer

### Game Plan

Take the opponent's best card with turn 1 discard, kill every threat on
sight with cheap removal, then land a resilient threat or planeswalker and
let card advantage bury them. No single synergy carries the deck; every card
is a good one-for-one trade. Stabilizes by turn 4 and wins around turns 7
to 9.

### Key Synergies

- **Discard into removal:** discard shows you what to answer, and removal
  keeps the board empty so one threat can win.
- **Fetch lands feed the graveyard:** Verdant Catacombs fills the graveyard
  for Tarmogoyf and Scavenging Ooze, triggers Tireless Tracker, and turns on
  Fatal Push's revolt.
- **Liliana of the Veil with discard:** once both hands are empty, her
  plus ability only hurts the opponent.
- **Kitchen Finks with Dark Confidant:** the life from Finks pays for
  Confidant's reveals.

### Discard

- **Thoughtseize:** look at their hand and take any nonland card. Cast it
  turn 1 to remove the card that beats you; it costs you 2 life.
- **Inquisition of Kozilek:** the same, but only mana value 3 or less. It
  costs no life, so it is the better turn 1 play against cheap-spell decks.

### Removal

- **Fatal Push:** kills a creature with mana value 2 or less for one mana,
  or up to 4 with revolt (crack a fetch land first). Best early against
  cheap threats.
- **Abrupt Decay:** destroys a nonland permanent with mana value 3 or less
  and cannot be countered. Use it on cheap threats, artifacts, enchantments,
  and planeswalkers against counterspell decks.
- **Assassin's Trophy:** destroys any permanent an opponent controls; they
  fetch a basic land. Save it for the big threat or a key artifact.
- **Maelstrom Pulse:** destroys any nonland permanent and all others with
  the same name. Best against tokens, copies, and go-wide boards.
- **Ravenous Chupacabra:** a creature that destroys an opposing creature
  when it enters. It answers large creatures that cheap removal cannot.

### Threats and Engines

- **Tarmogoyf:** grows with the card types in all graveyards. It is a
  large, cheap attacker once removal and fetch lands are in the graveyard.
- **Dark Confidant:** puts the top card into your hand each upkeep, and
  you lose life equal to that card's mana value. It wins grindy games; remove it if you are low on life.
- **Scavenging Ooze:** exiles cards from graveyards to grow and gain life.
  It also stops graveyard recursion decks.
- **Tireless Tracker:** investigates with each land and grows when you
  sacrifice Clues. It is the best long-game card advantage in the deck.
- **Kitchen Finks:** gains 2 life and comes back once with persist. Good
  against aggro and as a sacrifice-resistant blocker.

### Planeswalkers

- **Liliana of the Veil:** makes both players discard, or forces a
  sacrifice. Use the sacrifice ability on a lone threat.
- **Liliana, the Last Hope:** shrinks small attackers with her plus ability
  and returns creatures from the graveyard with her minus ability.

### Mana

- **Overgrown Tomb, Woodland Cemetery, Llanowar Wastes, Twilight Mire:**
  dual lands that make both colors from turn 1. Twilight Mire is a filter
  land.
- **Verdant Catacombs:** fetches any Swamp or Forest, including the dual
  lands, and fuels revolt and landfall.
- **Swamp and Forest:** the basic lands, which also give the fetches and
  Ooze targets.

### Mulligan

Keep hands with 2 to 4 lands and a discard spell or cheap removal. A hand
with Thoughtseize, a land of each color, and one threat is ideal. Mulligan
hands with no interaction and hands that only have expensive cards.

### Turn Guide

1. Turn 1: play a land and cast Thoughtseize, Inquisition of Kozilek, or
   Dark Confidant.
2. Turn 2: Fatal Push or another cheap answer, or a threat such as
   Tarmogoyf.
3. Turn 3: Liliana of the Veil, Tireless Tracker, or Kitchen Finks.
4. Turn 4: Ravenous Chupacabra, Liliana, the Last Hope, or two cheap spells
   in the same turn.
5. Turns 5 to 9: keep trading, protect Confidant and Tracker, and attack
   with Tarmogoyf while card advantage gives you every answer.

### Matchups/Cautions

- **Good against:** decks built around a few key cards (Voltron, combo),
  since discard and removal take those cards away.
- **Struggles against:** go-wide decks and decks that win through card
  quality (Superfriends), since one-for-one trades run out of answers.
- **Caution:** Dark Confidant can kill you against burn. Do not play it
  against fast aggro with a low life total.
