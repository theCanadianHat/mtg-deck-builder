# A4 Prowess: Notes

Part of the [archetype showcase](ARCHETYPES.md). Shared rules and power band
live there.

## Goals

- Cheap creatures that grow each time you cast a noncreature spell.
- Chain two or three cheap spells in one turn and a 1/2 becomes a 4/5.
- Wins around turns 4 to 5.

## Constraints

- Izzet (UR). Every creature rewards casting spells; every spell is cheap.
- No exclusions.

## Primer

### Game Plan

Deploy cheap creatures, then chain cheap spells in a single turn to grow
them. Cast spells during combat after blockers are declared, so every
trigger pumps a creature that is already unblocked. Wins around turns 4 to
5.

### Key Synergies

- **Prowess:** Monastery Swiftspear, Soul-Scar Mage, and Stormwing Entity
  get +1/+1 for each noncreature spell you cast that turn.
- **Sprite Dragon:** gets a permanent +1/+1 counter for each noncreature
  spell, so it grows every turn.
- **Free spells:** Manamorphose and Mutagenic Growth cost no net mana, so
  they add a trigger without slowing you down.
- **Cantrips:** Consider and Expressive Iteration replace themselves and
  keep the gas flowing.

### Threats

- **Monastery Swiftspear:** a 1/2 haste creature with prowess. Cast it on
  turn 1 for early damage.
- **Soul-Scar Mage:** a 1/2 with prowess. Damage from your spells to
  creatures your opponents control is dealt as -1/-1 counters instead.
  Burn on a blocker shrinks it permanently.
- **Sprite Dragon:** a 1/1 flying haste creature. It gets a +1/+1 counter
  whenever you cast a noncreature spell, and the counters stay.
- **Stormwing Entity:** a flying creature with prowess that scries when it
  enters. It costs less if you have already cast another instant or
  sorcery this turn, so cast a cheap spell first.

### Burn

- **Lightning Bolt:** 3 damage for {R}. Hold it for a blocker early and the
  face late.
- **Burst Lightning:** 2 damage for {R}, or 4 damage if you kick it for
  {4}. Use the kicker late in the game as removal or reach.
- **Lava Dart:** 1 damage to any target. Flashback by sacrificing a
  Mountain gives you a second spell, and a second prowess trigger, from
  one card.

### Cantrips and Pump

- **Consider:** look at the top card, you may put it into the graveyard,
  then draw a card. A one-mana trigger that smooths draws.
- **Expressive Iteration:** look at the top three cards, put one in your
  hand, one on the bottom, and exile one that you may play this turn only.
  Cast it with mana left over so you can use the exiled card.
- **Mutagenic Growth:** +2/+2 until end of turn, free if you pay 2 life.
  Use it in response to burn or in combat for a trigger.
- **Manamorphose:** adds two mana in any combination of colors and draws a
  card, so it effectively costs nothing. A free prowess trigger.
- **Temur Battle Rage:** gives a creature double strike, and trample if
  you have ferocious. A finisher on a creature already pumped by spells.
- **Spell Pierce:** counters a noncreature spell unless its controller
  pays {2}. Protects your plan from removal and sweepers.

### Mana

- **Steam Vents and Spirebluff Canal:** dual lands that make both colors.
  Spirebluff Canal enters untapped if you control two or fewer other
  lands.
- **Scalding Tarn:** fetches Steam Vents, Island, or Mountain, and thins the
  deck a little.
- **Island (4) and Mountain (3):** basic lands that fill out the colors.

### Mulligan

Keep hands with 2 to 3 lands, at least one prowess creature, and cheap
spells. Mulligan hands with no creatures or all expensive cards.

### Turn Guide

1. Turn 1: Monastery Swiftspear or Soul-Scar Mage, or a cantrip with a
   creature in play.
2. Turn 2: Sprite Dragon, plus a cheap spell during combat.
3. Turn 3: attack, then cast two or three cheap spells after blockers to
   pump. Expressive Iteration and Consider keep the hand full.
4. Turn 4: Stormwing Entity after a cheap spell, or burn to finish.
5. Turn 5: use Temur Battle Rage on your biggest unblocked creature.

### Matchups and Cautions

- **Good against:** slow decks (Big mana, Superfriends).
- **Struggles against:** cheap removal (Rock) and Hatebears' taxes.
- Hold burn for blockers early and the face late.
