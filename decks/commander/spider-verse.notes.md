# Spider-Verse: Notes

## Goals

- Commander: Cosmic Spider-Man (five-color). Spider-Verse tribal: the
  multiverse of Spider-heroes teaming up, inspired by _Into the Spider-Verse_.
- Only Spider Hero creatures from the Spider-Man sets, plus support cards.
  No generic spiders and no villains.
- Fun first. Target bracket 3 with low (bracket 2) and high swap options.

## Constraints

- Proxied, so no budget limit, but every card must earn its slot.
- Zero Game Changers in the main list.
- No infinite combos, extra turns, or mass land destruction.

## Movie cast

- Miles Morales: Miles Morales // Ultimate Spider-Man, Spider-Man, Miles
  Morales, Spider-Man, Brooklyn Visionary.
- Gwen Stacy: Gwen Stacy // Ghost-Spider, Ghost-Spider, Gwen Stacy,
  Spider-Gwen, Free Spirit.
- Peter B. Parker: Peter Parker // Amazing Spider-Man, Spider-Man, Peter
  Parker.
- Spider-Man Noir, Spider-Ham, SP//dr (Peni Parker), Spider-Man 2099 (both
  versions).
- _Across the Spider-Verse_: Spider-Punk, Spider-Man India, Spider-Woman,
  Scarlet Spider, Spider-Byte, Spider-UK, Spider-Rex, Spider-Girl, Masked
  Meower, Lady Spider, Sun-Spider.

## Primer

### Game Plan

Cast cheap Spider heroes on turns 1 to 4, then Cosmic Spider-Man. At the
beginning of combat on your turn, every other Spider you control gains
flying, first strike, trample, lifelink and haste, so the whole team
attacks in the air. Grow the team with +1/+1 counters, draw off every
Spider that enters or attacks, and recast Spiders with web-slinging for
more enter triggers. Wins around turns 7 to 9.

### Commander

- **Cosmic Spider-Man:** a 5/5 for {W}{U}{B}{R}{G} with flying, first
  strike, trample, lifelink and haste. Grants all five keywords to your
  other Spiders at the beginning of combat on your turn. Only Spiders get
  them: Human Citizen tokens do not, but Spider tokens and anything wearing
  Spider-Suit do.

### Cheap Spiders: Turns 1 to 3

These fill the board before the commander arrives. Every one is a Spider,
so all of them get Cosmic Spider-Man's keywords.

- **Spinneret and Spiderling:** a one-drop that grows whenever you attack
  with two or more Spiders. Once it deals 4 or more damage, exile the top
  card of your library to play, which also triggers Spider-Verse and
  Spider-Man 2099.
- **Masked Meower:** a hasty one-drop. Later, discard a card and sacrifice
  it to draw a card (which also triggers Lady Spider).
- **Miles Morales // Ultimate Spider-Man:** enters putting a +1/+1 counter
  on up to two creatures. Transform at sorcery speed for {3}{R}{G}{W} into
  a first strike, haste Spider that doubles every counter on your Spiders
  and legendary creatures whenever you attack.
- **Gwen Stacy // Ghost-Spider:** exiles your top card to play while you
  control her. Transformed, she is a flying, hasty Spider that grows when
  you play from exile and can exile more cards with her counters.
- **Peter Parker // Amazing Spider-Man:** makes a 2/1 Spider token with
  reach. Transformed, every colored legendary spell you cast has
  web-slinging {G}{W}{U}, which lets you recast your heroes cheaply.
- **Spider-Ham, Peter Porker:** the team lord. Other Spiders (and a long
  list of animals) get +1/+1. Also makes a Food.
- **Spider-Woman, Stunning Savior:** a 2/2 flier. Opposing artifacts and
  creatures enter tapped, so new blockers cannot block on the turn they
  arrive.
- **Spider-Punk:** riot, and gives riot to your other Spiders, so each new
  Spider enters with haste or a +1/+1 counter. Your spells and abilities
  cannot be countered, and damage cannot be prevented (beats Fog effects).
- **Spider-Girl, Legacy Hero:** a 2/2 with flying on your turn. Leaves a
  1/1 Citizen behind when it leaves.
- **Lady Spider, Maybelle Reilly:** puts a +1/+1 counter on a Spider
  whenever you discard. Pairs with Spider-Gwen and Masked Meower.
- **Spiders-Man, Heroic Horde:** a 2/3 for two. Cast with web-slinging
  for {4}{G}{G} late, it gains you 3 life and makes two 2/1 Spider tokens.
- **Spectacular Spider-Man:** a flash 3/2 that can fly for {1}. Sacrifice
  it for {1} to give your creatures hexproof and indestructible: your
  board-wipe insurance.
- **Skyward Spider:** a 2/2 with ward {2} that flies once it has a counter
  or Equipment.
- **Spider-Man 2099:** cannot be cast on your first three turns. A double
  strike 2/3; at your end step, if you cast a spell or played a land from
  anywhere other than your hand that turn, he deals damage equal to his
  power to any target. Casting your commander from the command zone
  counts.

### Web-Slinging and Enter Triggers

Web-slinging lets you cast a creature for a cheaper cost by returning a
tapped creature to hand. After combat, return a Spider that attacked, cast
a web-slinger cheaply, then recast the returned Spider for its enter
trigger.

- **Scarlet Spider, Ben Reilly:** web-slinging {R}{G}. A 4/3 trampler that
  enters with +1/+1 counters equal to the returned creature's mana value.
  Return your commander or a five-drop for a huge body.
- **Silk, Web Weaver:** web-slinging {1}{G}{W}. Makes a 1/1 Citizen
  whenever you cast a creature spell. {3}{G}{W} gives your creatures
  +2/+2 and vigilance.
- **Spider-UK:** web-slinging {2}{W}. Draws a card and gains 2 life at
  your end step if two or more creatures entered this turn.
- **Spider-Man, Brooklyn Visionary:** web-slinging {2}{G}. Fetches a basic
  land when it enters.
- **Spider-Man India:** web-slinging {1}{G}{W}. Whenever you cast a
  creature spell, put a +1/+1 counter on a creature and give it flying
  until end of turn.
- **Spider-Byte, Web Warden:** returns a nonland permanent to its owner's
  hand. Bounce a blocker, or rescue your own creature.
- **Spider-Man 2099, Miguel O'Hara:** returns a creature to hand when it
  enters, and draws a card whenever your creatures deal combat damage to a
  player.

### Counters and Modified Payoffs

Counters make your Spiders "modified", which turns on Skyward Spider,
Araña and SP//dr.

- **Araña, Heart of the Spider:** whenever you attack, a +1/+1 counter on
  an attacker. Whenever a modified creature deals combat damage to a
  player, exile the top card to play this turn.
- **SP//dr, Piloted by Peni:** a 4/4 vigilance artifact Spider that puts a
  counter on a creature. Draws a card whenever a modified creature deals
  combat damage to a player.
- **Virtuous Variant:** a 1/2 flier that puts a +1/+1 counter on a
  creature.
- **Web-Warriors:** a +1/+1 counter on each other creature you control.
- **Spider-Man, Miles Morales:** a 5/5 vigilance trampler. Whenever he
  enters or attacks, every other creature gets a +1/+1 counter and
  trample.
- **Spider-Man, Peter Parker:** a 4/4 flier. Whenever you gain life, put
  a +1/+1 counter on a creature and make it indestructible until end of
  turn. Each lifelinking Spider gives a separate trigger.
- **Spider-Man Noir:** a 4/4 menace. Whenever a creature attacks alone, it
  gets a +1/+1 counter and you surveil. Use it on turns when only Cosmic
  Spider-Man or one big Spider can attack safely.
- **Origin of Spider-Man:** a Saga. I makes a 2/1 Spider token, II puts a
  counter on a creature and makes it a legendary Spider Hero, III gives
  double strike for a turn.
- **Spider-Suit:** equipped creature gets +2/+2 and becomes a Spider Hero,
  so it gets Cosmic Spider-Man's keywords. Turns a Citizen token or a
  Food-holding non-Spider into a threat.
- **Sun-Spider, Nimble Webber:** flies on your turn. Fetches an Aura or
  Equipment when it enters; Spider-Suit is the only one in the list.

### Cast From Elsewhere

These reward casting spells from anywhere other than your hand.

- **Spider-Verse:** the legend rule does not apply to your Spiders. Once
  each turn, copy a spell you cast from anywhere other than your hand,
  and the copy of a permanent spell gains haste. Cast Cosmic Spider-Man
  from the command zone to get a second token copy. Cards exiled by Gwen
  Stacy, Spinneret and Araña also count.

### Card Advantage

- **Kindred Discovery:** name Spider. Draws a card whenever a Spider
  enters or attacks. The main draw engine.
- **Guardian Project:** draws when a nontoken creature enters with a name
  unlike any creature you control or in your graveyard. Every Spider has a
  different name, so it almost always draws.
- **Herald's Horn:** name Spider. Spiders cost {1} less, and each upkeep
  a Spider on top of your library goes to your hand.
- **Garruk's Uprising:** your creatures have trample. Draws when a
  creature with power 4 or greater enters.
- **Sensational Spider-Man:** when it attacks, tap an opposing creature
  and stun it. Then remove up to three stun counters from among all
  permanents to draw that many.
- **Spider-Gwen, Free Spirit:** loot whenever she becomes tapped (attack
  or crew Spider-Mobile). Discards trigger Lady Spider.
- **Return of the Wildspeaker:** draw cards equal to the greatest power
  among your non-Human creatures, or give non-Humans +3/+3. Most of your
  Spiders, including Cosmic Spider-Man, are Human, so count only Spider
  tokens, Spider-Ham, Spiders-Man, Masked Meower, Spider-Byte, Spider-Rex,
  SP//dr and Web-Warriors.

### Finishers

- **Ghost-Spider, Gwen Stacy:** a 4/4 menace. Whenever she attacks, she
  deals damage to the defending player equal to the number of attackers.
- **Spider-Man, Web-Spinner:** a hasty double strike 2/3. Whenever he
  attacks, a creature cannot block.
- **Spider-Rex, Daring Dino:** a 6/6 reach trampler with ward {2}.
- **Spider-Mobile:** a 3/3 trample Vehicle (crew 2) that gets +1/+1 for
  each Spider you control when it attacks or blocks. It is not a Spider,
  so it does not get Cosmic Spider-Man's keywords.

### Tappers

- **Spider-Woman, Secret Agent:** flash. Taps an opposing creature that
  stays tapped while you control her. Lock down their best blocker.

### Protection

- **Spider-Man, To the Rescue:** flash. Tap him on entry to make another
  nonattacking creature indestructible. Answers targeted removal on
  Cosmic Spider-Man.
- **Heroic Intervention:** your permanents gain hexproof and
  indestructible until end of turn.
- **Boros Charm:** 4 damage to a player, indestructible for your
  permanents, or double strike on one creature. Indestructible saves the
  board from a wipe; double strike on a lifelink flier ends games.

### Ramp

- **Sol Ring:** {C}{C} from turn one.
- **Arcane Signet:** any color in your identity.
- **Chromatic Lantern:** your lands tap for any color. Fixes five-color
  costs such as Cosmic Spider-Man.
- **Farseek:** a Plains, Island, Swamp or Mountain card (including shocks
  and tri-lands) onto the battlefield tapped.
- **Nature's Lore:** a Forest card onto the battlefield untapped. Shocks
  and tri-lands with the Forest type are legal targets.
- **Three Visits:** a second copy of Nature's Lore.
- **Cultivate:** one basic onto the battlefield tapped, one to hand.
- **Kodama's Reach:** a second copy of Cultivate.

### Interaction

- **Swords to Plowshares:** exile a creature for {W}.
- **Path to Exile:** exile a creature for {W}; its controller gets a
  basic land.
- **Counterspell:** counter a board wipe or a combo piece.
- **Assassin's Trophy:** destroy any permanent an opponent controls.
- **Beast Within:** destroy any permanent; its controller gets a 3/3.
- **Generous Gift:** destroy any permanent; its controller gets a 3/3.
- **Chaos Warp:** shuffle any permanent into its owner's library.
- **Anguished Unmaking:** exile a nonland permanent for 3 life.

### Mana Base

36 lands and Chromatic Lantern. Cavern of Souls, Secluded Courtyard and
Unclaimed Territory (name Spider) and Plaza of Heroes make colored mana
for your creatures. Five tri-lands, ten shocks and four fetch lands cover
five colors; the fetches find the shocks and tri-lands by land type.

### Mulligan

Keep hands with 3 or more lands (or 2 lands plus Sol Ring or Arcane
Signet) and at least one cheap Spider. A hand with three or more Spiders
is worth keeping with fewer lands. Mulligan hands that cannot make three
colors by turn 4.

### Turn Guide

1. **Turns 1-3:** cheap Spiders and a rock. Spider-Ham or Spider-Punk early
   makes every later Spider better. Kindred Discovery or Guardian Project
   on turn 3-5 if you have it.
2. **Turn 4-5:** Cosmic Spider-Man. At the beginning of combat, every other
   Spider gets flying, first strike, trample, lifelink and haste.
3. **Turns 5-7:** after combat, web-sling a Spider to return a tapped one,
   then recast it. Cast Spider-Verse before recasting the commander from
   the command zone.
4. **Turns 7-9:** attack in the air with the whole team. Add counters with
   Spider-Man, Miles Morales or Web-Warriors and finish with Boros Charm
   or Ghost-Spider, Gwen Stacy.

### Cautions

- The team only has evasion while Cosmic Spider-Man is on the battlefield
  at the beginning of combat. Protect him with Spider-Man, To the Rescue,
  Heroic Intervention or Spectacular Spider-Man.
- Do not overextend into a board wipe; hold Spectacular Spider-Man or
  Heroic Intervention up first.
- Spider-Man 2099 cannot be cast on your first three turns.
- Return of the Wildspeaker only counts and pumps non-Human creatures.

## Power options

### Low: bracket 2 swaps

| Cut | Add | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sol Ring | Commander's Sphere | Slower start |
| Kindred Discovery | Spider-Man, Web-Slinger | Less card draw |
| Guardian Project | Wall Crawl | Less card draw |
| Anguished Unmaking | Thwip! | Softer interaction |

### High: upper bracket 3 swaps

These add three Game Changers, the bracket 3 limit.

| Cut | Add | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Origin of Spider-Man | Smothering Tithe | Big mana |
| Boros Charm | Teferi's Protection | Full protection |
| Counterspell | Cyclonic Rift | One-sided board reset |
| Garruk's Uprising | Akroma's Will | Alpha strike finisher |
