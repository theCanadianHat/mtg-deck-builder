# R1 Big Mana: Notes

Part of the [archetype showcase](ARCHETYPES.md). Shared rules and power band
live there.

## Goals

- Show the ramp lane: make far more mana than normal, then cast huge threats
  early.
- Lands a 6-drop on turn 4 and wins around turns 6 to 8.

## Constraints

- Mono-green with colorless Eldrazi. Every card makes mana, finds mana, or
  pays it off.
- No exclusions.

## Primer

### Game Plan

Play mana creatures and ramp spells on turns 1 to 3, then cast a six-drop on
turn 4 and a game-ending threat on turn 5 or 6. Every card makes mana, finds
mana, or pays it off. Wins around turns 6 to 8.

### Key Synergies

- **Ramp into payoffs:** Llanowar Elves, Elvish Mystic, Utopia Sprawl, and
  Sakura-Tribe Elder put you two to three mana ahead.
- **Nissa with Forests:** Nissa, Who Shakes the World doubles Forest mana
  and, with a deck of 21 Forests, the mana is enormous.
- **Green Sun's Zenith as a tutor:** finds Craterhoof, Hornet Queen, or
  any green creature you need.
- **Nykthos with a green board:** Nykthos, Shrine to Nyx turns the green
  permanents you control into a burst of green mana.

### Mana Creatures and Ramp

- **Llanowar Elves:** a turn 1 mana creature that taps for green.
- **Elvish Mystic:** the same effect. Four copies of each make a turn 1
  ramp more likely.
- **Sakura-Tribe Elder:** blocks, then is sacrificed for a basic land, so it
  ramps and protects you from an early attacker.
- **Utopia Sprawl:** an Aura on a Forest that adds an extra mana. It is a
  turn 1 play that does not die to sweepers.
- **Cultivate:** fetches two basic lands, one onto the battlefield and one
  into your hand. It ramps and fixes your land drops.
- **Oracle of Mul Daya:** plays an extra land each turn and lets you play
  lands from the top of your library. It ramps and gives card advantage.

### Tutors and Payoffs

- **Green Sun's Zenith:** finds any green creature, with X as its mana
  value. Fetch a mana creature early or Craterhoof late.
- **Nissa, Who Shakes the World:** makes extra mana from Forests and turns
  a land into a 3/3 attacker each turn.
- **Primeval Titan:** a trampler that fetches two lands when it enters or
  attacks. Fetch Nykthos and Castle Garenbrig for big mana.
- **Genesis Wave:** puts permanents with mana value X or less from the top
  of your library onto the battlefield. It is a huge payoff with 6 or more
  mana.

### Finishers

- **Craterhoof Behemoth:** has haste itself, and when it enters your creatures
  get +X/+X and trample, where X is the number of creatures you control.
  Creatures that came in this turn still can't attack, so build the board
  first. It wins the game
  from a wide board.
- **Hornet Queen:** makes four 1/1 deathtouch flying Insects, and is a
  great defense against large attackers and flyers.
- **Wurmcoil Engine:** a deathtouch, lifelink body that leaves two 3/3
  tokens when it dies. It is the best answer to aggro.
- **Ulamog, the Ceaseless Hunger:** exiles two permanents when you cast it,
  is indestructible, and exiles the top twenty cards of the defending
  player's library when it attacks.
- **Worldspine Wurm:** a huge trampler that leaves three 5/5 Wurms when it
  dies, and shuffles into your library.

### Mana Sinks and Utility Lands

- **Castle Garenbrig:** a land that adds six green mana for creature spells
  and abilities, so it powers Craterhoof or Hornet Queen.
- **Nykthos, Shrine to Nyx:** makes extra green mana from devotion, so cast
  it when you have a heavy green board.
- **Forest:** 21 copies make the mana for Nissa and keep the deck simple.

### Mulligan

Keep hands with 2 or 3 lands and a mana creature or ramp spell, or one land
with several mana creatures. A hand with a turn 1 Elf and a Cultivate is
excellent. Mulligan hands with no way to ramp and only expensive spells.

### Turn Guide

1. Turn 1: land, then Llanowar Elves, Elvish Mystic, or Utopia Sprawl.
2. Turn 2: Sakura-Tribe Elder, Cultivate, or a second ramp piece.
3. Turn 3: Oracle of Mul Daya, Nissa, or Green Sun's Zenith for a mana
   creature, to reach four to six mana.
4. Turn 4: Primeval Titan, Wurmcoil Engine, or Hornet Queen.
5. Turns 5 to 8: Craterhoof Behemoth, Ulamog, Worldspine Wurm, or a big
   Genesis Wave to finish the game.

### Matchups/Cautions

- **Good against:** midrange and fair creature decks, since your threats are
  larger than theirs.
- **Struggles against:** fast aggro, counterspells, and Prison.
- **Caution:** do not cast Genesis Wave with only a few lands. Mana
  creatures die to sweepers and cheap removal, so keep a second ramp piece.
