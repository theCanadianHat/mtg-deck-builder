# M3 Blink: Notes

Part of the [archetype showcase](ARCHETYPES.md). Shared rules and power band
live there.

## Goals

- Show the midrange lane built on enter-the-battlefield (ETB) value and
  cards that exile and return creatures to repeat it.
- Stabilizes around turn 4 and wins around turns 8 to 10.

## Constraints

- Azorius (WU). Every creature has an ETB or blinks.
- No exclusions.

## Primer

### Game Plan

Play creatures with strong enter-the-battlefield effects, plus cards that
exile and return them so the effect happens again. Every blink is a free
spell. Blink in response to removal: the creature dodges it and triggers
again. Stabilizes around turn 4 and wins around turns 8 to 10.

### Key Synergies

- **Blink in response to removal:** the creature dodges the spell and its
  ETB triggers again.
- **Ephemerate's rebound:** gives a second blink on your next upkeep.
- **Solitude:** evoke it by exiling a white card for a free removal spell,
  then blink it in response to the sacrifice so you keep it.
- **Soulherder and Thassa:** both blink a creature every end step, so any
  ETB creature repeats its effect each turn.

### Blink Spells

- **Ephemerate:** a one-mana instant that exiles and returns your creature.
  It protects it from removal and rebounds next turn for another trigger.
- **Momentary Blink:** the same effect as an instant, with flashback from
  the graveyard for a second use.

### Blinkers and Flicker Creatures

- **Soulherder:** at your end step, it blinks a creature you control. It
  also grows whenever a creature is exiled from the battlefield.
- **Flickerwisp:** a 3/1 flyer whose ETB exiles another permanent until the
  next end step. Use it to reset a blocker, remove a token, or blink your
  own ETB creature.
- **Restoration Angel:** a flash flyer that blinks a non-Angel creature you
  control. Cast it in response to removal or at the end of the opponent's
  turn.
- **Thassa, Deep-Dwelling:** an indestructible blinker that blinks one of
  your creatures at your end step.

### ETB Creatures

- **Skyclave Apparition:** exiles a nonland, nontoken permanent with mana
  value 4 or less. It is your main answer to cheap threats and artifacts.
- **Solitude:** a flash lifelinker that exiles a creature when it enters.
  Cast it at instant speed for removal, or evoke it for free.
- **Wall of Omens:** a 0/4 blocker that draws a card, so it stabilizes
  against aggro and replaces itself.
- **Mulldrifter:** a flyer that draws two cards. It can also be evoked for
  three mana for the same draw, then blinked to keep it.
- **Spirited Companion:** a cheap creature that draws a card and gives a
  body to blink.
- **Charming Prince:** a flexible ETB (scry 2, gain 3 life, or blink another
  creature of yours) that fits any stage of the game.

### Mana

- **Hallowed Fountain, Glacial Fortress, Mystic Gate:** dual lands that make
  both colors. Mystic Gate is a filter land.
- **Flooded Strand:** fetches a Plains or Island, including Hallowed
  Fountain.
- **Plains and Island:** the basic lands. White is the main color.

### Mulligan

Keep hands with 2 to 4 lands and at least one ETB creature or a blinker
with a creature to target. A hand with only blink spells and no creatures is
a mulligan.

### Turn Guide

1. Turn 1: play a land. The list has no one-drop creatures, so hold up
   nothing and plan turn 2.
2. Turn 2: Spirited Companion, Wall of Omens, or Charming Prince.
3. Turn 3: Skyclave Apparition, Mulldrifter, or Soulherder, or hold
   Ephemerate for removal.
4. Turn 4: Restoration Angel, Flickerwisp, or Solitude (hard cast or
   evoke) to answer their threat.
5. Turns 5 to 10: blink the best ETB creature every turn, grow Soulherder,
   and win with flyers while the opponent runs out of answers.

### Matchups/Cautions

- **Good against:** fair creature decks (Rock, Counters), since you have
  more card advantage and removal on every body.
- **Struggles against:** fast combo and counterspell decks.
- **Caution:** keep a blink spell back for removal when you can. Using it
  only for value leaves the creature exposed.
