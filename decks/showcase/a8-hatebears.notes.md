# A8 Hatebears: Notes

Part of the [archetype showcase](ARCHETYPES.md). Shared rules and power band
live there.

## Goals

- Small white creatures whose abilities tax or stop the opponent.
- Attack for a few points each turn while they pay extra for everything.
- Wins around turns 6 to 7.

## Constraints

- Mono-white. Every creature disrupts or generates value.
- No exclusions.

## Primer

### Game Plan

Lead with taxes against spell-heavy decks and with Stoneforge Mystic against
creature decks. Attack for a few points each turn while the opponent pays
extra for everything, and use land denial to keep them off their colors.
Wins around turns 6 to 7.

### Key Synergies

- **Aether Vial and Thalia:** Vial puts creatures into play at instant
  speed, so you can dodge your own Thalia tax.
- **Stoneforge Mystic:** finds Batterskull or Sword of Fire and Ice and
  puts it into play cheaply.
- **Flicker value:** Flickerwisp, Blade Splicer, Skyclave Apparition, and
  Brutal Cathar all have enters-the-battlefield effects.
- **Land denial:** Ghost Quarter and Rishadan Port deny mana. With Leonin
  Arbiter in play, Ghost Quarter becomes land destruction, because the
  opponent must pay {2} to search.

### Taxes and Disruption

- **Thalia, Guardian of Thraben:** a 2/1 first strike creature. Noncreature
  spells cost {1} more for everyone. Best against spell-heavy decks.
- **Leonin Arbiter:** a 2/2 that stops library searches unless the player
  pays {2}. It hurts fetch lands and tutors.
- **Archon of Emeria:** a flying creature. Each player can cast only one
  spell per turn, and nonbasic lands opponents control enter tapped.
  Cast it when the opponent relies on chaining spells.
- **Phyrexian Revoker:** you name a card, and activated abilities of
  sources with that name cannot be activated. Name a key engine or
  planeswalker.

### Value Creatures

- **Flickerwisp:** a flying creature that exiles another target permanent
  when it enters, and it returns at the next end step. Use it to remove a
  blocker for a turn or to reset a token.
- **Blade Splicer:** makes a 3/3 Golem artifact token when it enters. Golems
  have first strike while Blade Splicer is in play.
- **Skyclave Apparition:** exiles a nonland, nontoken permanent with mana
  value 4 or less an opponent controls when it enters. Best on their
  first good permanent.
- **Brutal Cathar:** exiles a creature an opponent controls when it enters
  or transforms, until it leaves the battlefield. Removal on a body.
- **Stoneforge Mystic:** searches for an Equipment when it enters. {1}{W},
  {T}: put an Equipment from your hand onto the battlefield.

### Equipment

- **Batterskull:** comes with a 0/0 Germ that gets +4/+4, vigilance, and
  lifelink. It can return to your hand for {3}. A hard-to-remove threat.
- **Sword of Fire and Ice:** +2/+2 and protection from red and blue. When
  the creature deals combat damage, it deals 2 damage to any target and
  you draw a card.

### Removal and Mana

- **Path to Exile:** exiles a creature, and its controller searches for a
  basic land. Use it on their best creature.
- **Aether Vial:** play it on turn 1, and tap it to put a creature into
  play at instant speed. Creatures dodge counterspells this way, and Vial
  keeps deploying after Thalia taxes noncreature spells.
- **Ghost Quarter:** sacrifice it to destroy any target land. Its
  controller may search for a basic land. Use it on a key dual or utility land.
- **Rishadan Port:** a land that taps a target land for {1} and {T}. Use it
  on their upkeep or end step to keep them off mana.
- **Plains (15):** basic lands for a mono-white deck.

### Mulligan

Keep hands with 2 to 4 lands and a one- or two-mana creature. Stoneforge
Mystic or Thalia is a strong keep. Mulligan hands with no creature or
only equipment.

### Turn Guide

1. Turn 1: Aether Vial or Thalia, Guardian of Thraben.
2. Turn 2: Leonin Arbiter, Stoneforge Mystic, or Phyrexian Revoker.
3. Turn 3: Flickerwisp, Blade Splicer, Archon of Emeria, or Skyclave
   Apparition. Use Stoneforge to put an Equipment into play.
4. Turn 4: Brutal Cathar or equip Sword of Fire and Ice. Use Ghost
   Quarter or Rishadan Port at the right time.
5. Turns 6 to 7: attack with the team, with Batterskull or Sword.

### Matchups and Cautions

- **Good against:** Storm, Prowess, and combo decks that need many spells.
- **Struggles against:** sweepers and bigger creatures.
- Lead with taxes against spell-heavy decks; lead with Stoneforge against
  creature decks.
