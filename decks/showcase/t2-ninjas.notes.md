# T2 Ninjas: Notes

Part of the [archetype showcase](ARCHETYPES.md). Shared rules and power band
live there.

## Goals

- Show the tempo lane of cheap unblockable creatures that swap into bigger
  Ninjas, drawing cards or stealing value with each hit.
- Wins around turns 6 to 7.

## Constraints

- Dimir (UB). Every creature is evasive or a Ninja.
- No exclusions.

## Primer

### Game Plan

Attack with cheap evasive creatures, then use ninjutsu: return the unblocked
attacker to your hand and put a Ninja into play attacking. Each Ninja draws
cards or steals value when it connects. Ninjutsu happens after blockers are
declared, so wait until a creature is unblocked, then pay and swap. Wins
around turns 6 to 7.

### Key Synergies

- **Unblockable bodies into Ninjas:** every evasive creature is also a way
  to cast a Ninja for cheap.
- **Replaying enter-the-battlefield effects:** returning Faerie Seer to your
  hand lets you replay it to scry again.
- **Ninja card draw:** Ingenious Infiltrator draws a card whenever any Ninja
  you control deals combat damage, so a team of Ninjas draws a lot.
- **Tetsuko Umezawa:** makes creatures with power or toughness 1 or less
  unblockable, which covers most of your evasive bodies and the 1/x Ninjas.

### Evasive Bodies

- **Faerie Seer:** a 1/1 flyer that scries 2 when it enters. Bounce and
  replay it with ninjutsu for more scry.
- **Spectral Sailor:** a flash flyer that can draw cards late with its
  ability. It can be cast at the end of the opponent's turn.
- **Changeling Outcast:** can't block or be blocked, and counts as a Ninja
  because of changeling. It is a reliable unblocked attacker.
- **Tetsuko Umezawa, Fugitive:** a body that makes small creatures
  unblockable. Cast it before you attack with several creatures.

### Ninjas

- **Moon-Circuit Hacker:** a cheap ninjutsu that loots you when it connects,
  with no discard if it entered this turn. It is the best early swap.
- **Ninja of the Deep Hours:** draws a card whenever it deals combat damage
  to a player. Ninjutsu it in on turn 2 or 3.
- **Ingenious Infiltrator:** draws a card whenever any Ninja you control
  deals combat damage, so it is the engine of the deck.
- **Mist-Syndicate Naga:** makes a token copy of itself whenever it deals
  combat damage to a player. It builds a team of Ninjas.
- **Fallen Shinobi:** a big Ninja that exiles the opponent's top two cards
  when it connects, and lets you play them for free. It is a late-game
  swing.

### Planeswalker

- **Kaito Shizuki:** phases out the turn it enters, draws cards, and makes
  unblockable 1/1 Ninja tokens with its minus ability. It is a card engine
  that protects itself.

### Interaction

- **Fatal Push:** kills a cheap creature, usually a blocker or a threat,
  for one mana. Use it to clear the way for an attack.
- **Counterspell:** holds up the opponent's removal or sweeper. Cast it on
  their turn when you do not need the mana.
- **Spell Pierce:** counters a noncreature spell unless they pay 2. Best
  against removal, early.

### Cantrips

- **Consider:** looks at the top card, then draws a card. It smooths your
  draws and finds a Ninja or an evasive body.

### Mana

- **Watery Grave, Darkslick Shores:** dual lands that make both colors.
- **Polluted Delta:** fetches an Island or Swamp, including Watery Grave.
- **Island and Swamp:** the basic lands. Blue is the main color.

### Mulligan

Keep hands with 2 to 4 lands, an evasive creature, and a Ninja or a cheap
interaction spell. A hand with Spectral Sailor, Moon-Circuit Hacker, and
lands is ideal. Mulligan hands with only Ninjas and no creature to swap.

### Turn Guide

1. Turn 1: Faerie Seer, Spectral Sailor, or Changeling Outcast.
2. Turn 2: attack and ninjutsu Moon-Circuit Hacker or Ninja of the Deep
   Hours, or cast another evasive creature.
3. Turn 3: Tetsuko Umezawa, Kaito Shizuki, or ninjutsu Naga or Ingenious
   Infiltrator, with Fatal Push or Counterspell mana open.
4. Turn 4: ninjutsu Fallen Shinobi or replay your evasive creature, then
   attack again.
5. Turns 5 to 7: keep swapping Ninjas, draw cards, and win with unblocked
   attacks.

### Matchups/Cautions

- **Good against:** slow control and decks with few flyers, since your
  creatures are hard to block.
- **Struggles against:** fast aggro (Burn) and decks with many cheap
  blockers.
- **Caution:** do not ninjutsu in without an unblocked attacker. Hold Fatal
  Push or Counterspell for the card that would stop your Ninja.
