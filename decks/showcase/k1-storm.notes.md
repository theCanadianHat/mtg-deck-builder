# K1 Storm: Notes

Part of the [archetype showcase](ARCHETYPES.md). Shared rules and power band
live there.

## Constraints

- Izzet (UR). Every card adds mana, reduces costs, digs, or finishes.
- No exclusions.

## Primer

### Game Plan

Cast many cheap spells in one turn, using rituals to net mana, then finish
with Grapeshot (damage per spell cast) or Empty the Warrens (Goblins per
spell). Wins around turns 4 to 5.

### Key Synergies

- Cost reducers make every cheap spell cheaper, so rituals and cantrips
  chain into more spells than mana spent.
- Rituals and Manamorphose raise the storm count while netting mana.
- Past in Flames lets you replay the spells already cast this turn, doubling
  your storm count for the finisher.
- Gifts Ungiven finds Grapeshot and Past in Flames in one card.

### Cost Reducers

- **Baral, Chief of Compliance:** makes instants and sorceries cost {1}
  less, and lets you loot when your spell counters one.
- **Goblin Electromancer:** instants and sorceries cost {1} less; the
  main engine in a hand without Baral.
- **Ral, Monsoon Mage:** a third cost reducer: instants and sorceries cost
  {1} less. Each instant or sorcery on your turn flips a coin (lose: 1
  damage to you; win: it may transform into Ral, Leyline Prodigy). Treat
  the planeswalker side as a bonus.

### Rituals

- **Pyretic Ritual:** an instant that adds {R}{R}{R} for two mana, a net
  gain of one mana.
- **Desperate Ritual:** the same net-plus-one mana instant.
- **Manamorphose:** costs one mana, gives two mana, and draws a card, so it
  is free and raises the storm count.

### Cantrips and Dig

- **Sleight of Hand:** looks at the top two cards and takes one; cheap dig.
- **Serum Visions:** draws a card and scries 2; sets up the next turns.
- **Reckless Impulse:** exiles the top two cards and lets you play them
  until the end of your next turn; refuels the hand.

### Setup and Interaction

- **Remand:** counters a spell, returns it to hand, and draws; protects
  the combo turn or buys time against a key spell.
- **Gifts Ungiven:** search for four cards with different names; the
  opponent puts two in your graveyard and you get the other two. Pick
  Grapeshot, Past in Flames, Empty the Warrens and a ritual: either split
  gives you a kill, because Past in Flames can flash back a binned
  Grapeshot or ritual.

### Finishers

- **Grapeshot:** deals 1 damage per spell cast this turn; copies hit any
  target. The main kill spell.
- **Empty the Warrens:** makes two Goblins for each spell cast this turn;
  a backup when the opponent has lifegain or a blocker.
- **Past in Flames:** gives instants and sorceries in your graveyard
  flashback; cast it mid-turn for a second wave of spells.

### Mana

- **Steam Vents (4), Spirebluff Canal (2):** dual lands that give both
  colors on turn 1 or 2.
- **Scalding Tarn (4):** fetches Steam Vents, Island, or Mountain; thins
  the deck.
- **Island (5) and Mountain (4):** basic lands for the two colors.

### Mulligan

Keep hands with 2 to 4 lands, a reducer or ritual, and at least two cheap
spells. Mulligan hands without lands, or ones with only the finishers.

### Turn Guide

1. Turn 1: land, then a cantrip such as Serum Visions or Sleight of Hand.
2. Turn 2: Goblin Electromancer or Baral; hold Remand if possible.
3. Turn 3: cantrips and rituals to dig toward the finisher; wait for a
   turn with enough fuel.
4. Turn 4-5: go off: chain rituals, cantrips, and Past in Flames, then
   finish with Grapeshot or Empty the Warrens.

### Matchups/Cautions

- **Good against:** slow decks with no counterspells.
- **Struggles against:** Hatebears, Prison, and Draw-go.
- **Caution:** count mana, cards in hand, and the storm needed before you
  start; storm counts every spell cast this turn, including the
  opponent's.
