# A3 Tribal Lords: Notes

Part of the [archetype showcase](ARCHETYPES.md). Shared rules and power band
live there.

## Goals

- Merfolk, all the way down. Lords give every other Merfolk +1/+1, so
  each creature makes the rest bigger.
- Islandwalk makes the team unblockable against anyone with an Island.
- Wins around turns 5 to 6.

## Constraints

- Mono-blue. Every creature is a Merfolk or a Merfolk payoff.
- No exclusions.

## Primer

### Game Plan

Curve out Merfolk, stack lords, and attack. Each lord pumps the rest of
the team, and islandwalk makes them unblockable against any opponent who
controls an Island. Spreading Seas turns an opposing land into an Island to
turn islandwalk on. Wins around turns 5 to 6.

### Key Synergies

- **Lords:** every lord pumps every other Merfolk, so two lords on a board
  of four Merfolk is a large attack.
- **Aether Vial:** puts Merfolk into play at instant speed, past
  counterspells and sorcery-speed removal.
- **Islandwalk:** Lord of Atlantis and Master of the Pearl Trident give
  Merfolk islandwalk, and Spreading Seas turns a land into an Island.
- **Mutavault:** it has every creature type, so lords pump it and it
  counts as a Merfolk.

### Merfolk Creatures

- **Kumena's Speaker:** a 1/1 for {G} that is 2/2 while you control another
  Merfolk or an Island. Warning: it costs green and the deck has only
  Islands and Mutavault, so it can only enter through Aether Vial on 1.
  See Cautions.
- **Silvergill Adept:** a Merfolk that draws a card when it enters. Its
  extra cost is revealing a Merfolk card from your hand or paying {3}, so
  keep a second Merfolk in hand when you cast it.
- **Merfolk Trickster:** a flash Merfolk that taps a creature and strips
  its abilities for a turn when it enters. Cast it on their attack to
  ambush an attacker, or on your turn to tap a blocker.
- **Harbinger of the Tides:** when it enters, return a tapped creature an
  opponent controls to its owner's hand. Pay {2} more to cast it with
  flash on their turn and bounce an attacker, or cast it on your turn after
  they attacked to clear a blocker.
- **Master of Waves:** protection from red. When it enters, it makes a 1/0
  Elemental for each {U} in the mana costs of your permanents (your
  devotion to blue), and Elementals get +1/+1. With two lords out, that is
  a big late-game swing.

### Lords

- **Lord of Atlantis:** other Merfolk get +1/+1 and islandwalk. Play it
  after a Merfolk so it is not alone.
- **Master of the Pearl Trident:** other Merfolk get +1/+1 and islandwalk.
  Another lord that is worth holding until you can play two creatures.
- **Merrow Reejerey:** other Merfolk get +1/+1. Whenever you cast a Merfolk
  spell, you may tap or untap target permanent, so it can tap a blocker or
  untap a land.
- **Svyelun of Sea and Sky:** not a pump lord. It gives other Merfolk ward
  {1}, draws a card each time it attacks, and is indestructible while you
  control two other Merfolk. It protects the lords from removal.

### Mana and Disruption

- **Aether Vial:** play it on turn 1 and set its counter to 2 for most of
  the game. Tap it to put a Merfolk with mana value equal to its counters
  into play.
- **Spreading Seas:** an Aura that turns a target land into an Island and
  draws you a card. Use it on an opponent's land to enable islandwalk.
- **Spell Pierce:** counters a noncreature spell unless its controller pays
  {2}. Use it on removal or sweepers, early in the game.
- **Mutavault:** a land that becomes a 2/2 creature for {1} until end of
  turn. It has every creature type, so it benefits from lords.
- **Island (16):** the only basic land. Every spell except Kumena's
  Speaker is blue.

### Mulligan

Keep hands with 2 to 4 lands and a one- or two-mana Merfolk. Aether Vial
is a strong keep. Mulligan hands with only lords or no Merfolk.

### Turn Guide

1. Turn 1: Aether Vial.
2. Turn 2: Silvergill Adept or Merfolk Trickster, or a second Merfolk
   with Vial at 2.
3. Turn 3: two creatures if possible, or a lord on a board with Merfolk.
   Spreading Seas on an opposing land if they have no Island.
4. Turn 4: Merrow Reejerey and Merfolk, or a flash creature at the end
   of their turn.
5. Turns 5 to 6: lords plus islandwalk attack. Use Mutavault, Trickster,
   and Harbinger to remove blockers and finish.

### Matchups and Cautions

- **Good against:** blue decks (islandwalk) and slow control.
- **Struggles against:** sweepers and decks with bigger creatures (Rock,
  Big mana).
- Hold lords until you can play two creatures in a turn. A single lord
  dies to removal and leaves the team small.
- Kumena's Speaker costs {G}, but the deck has no green source. Until the
  list changes, the only way to play it is Aether Vial on 1. Swapping it
  for a blue one-drop Merfolk would fix this.
