# K4 Two-card Infinite: Notes

Part of the [archetype showcase](ARCHETYPES.md). Shared rules and power band
live there.

## Constraints

- Izzet (UR). Every card is a combo piece, protects it, or finds it.
- No exclusions.

## Primer

### Game Plan

Two cards win on the spot. Splinter Twin or Kiki-Jiki copies Pestermite or
Deceiver Exarch; the copy untaps the original, which copies again, for
infinite hasty attackers. The rest of the deck plays like tempo until the
combo is safe. Wins around turns 4 to 6.

### Key Synergies

- Pestermite and Deceiver Exarch have flash, so you can play them at the
  end of the opponent's turn, then go off on your turn with Splinter Twin.
- Kiki-Jiki has haste, so it can copy Pestermite, Exarch, or Zealous
  Conscripts the turn it arrives.
- Counterspells and Remand protect the combo on the key turn.

### Combo Enablers

- **Splinter Twin:** an Aura that gives the creature a tap ability to
  create a hasty copy. On Pestermite or Exarch, the copy untaps the
  original for infinite copies.
- **Kiki-Jiki, Mirror Breaker:** a hasty creature that taps to copy a
  nonlegendary creature; with Pestermite or Exarch, it untaps for the
  loop.

### Combo Creatures

- **Pestermite:** a flash flyer that taps or untaps a permanent when it
  enters; the untap is what makes the loop work.
- **Deceiver Exarch:** the same combo piece with a sturdier body; its
  enters trigger untaps the creature that copied it.
- **Zealous Conscripts:** a hasty creature that steals a permanent until
  end of turn when it enters; with Kiki-Jiki, its copies loop for the win.

### Interaction and Protection

- **Counterspell:** stops removal or a threat when you hold {U}{U} up.
- **Remand:** counters a spell and returns it to hand while drawing a card;
  buys a turn against a key spell.
- **Spell Pierce:** counters a noncreature spell unless they pay {2};
  protects Splinter Twin and answers removal.
- **Izzet Charm:** a flexible instant: counter a noncreature spell unless
  they pay {2}, deal 2 damage to a creature, or draw two cards then
  discard two.
- **Lightning Bolt:** cheap removal for an early blocker or answers an
  aggressive creature while you hold the combo.

### Setup and Value

- **Serum Visions:** draws and scries to find the combo pieces.
- **Sleight of Hand:** digs two cards deep for a combo piece.
- **Snapcaster Mage:** a flash creature that gives a spell in your graveyard
  flashback; replays a counterspell or Bolt.

### Mana

- **Steam Vents (4), Sulfur Falls (2), Spirebluff Canal (2):** dual lands.
- **Scalding Tarn (4):** a fetch that finds a dual or a basic.
- **Island (7) and Mountain (5):** basic lands.

### Mulligan

Keep hands with 2 to 4 lands and a combo piece with protection, or strong
tempo cards. Mulligan hands without lands or with only a combo enabler and
no copy target.

### Turn Guide

1. Turn 1: land and Serum Visions or Sleight of Hand.
2. Turn 2: Lightning Bolt a threat, or hold Remand or Spell Pierce.
3. Turn 3: flash in Pestermite or Exarch at the end of their turn.
4. Turn 4: Splinter Twin on the creature, or Kiki-Jiki, if protection is up;
   copy infinitely and attack.
5. Turns 5-6: if they have removal up, play fair with tempo and wait.

### Matchups/Cautions

- **Good against:** slow decks that cannot interact at instant speed.
- **Struggles against:** instant-speed removal and Hatebears' Revoker.
- **Caution:** if they have removal up, play fair: tap their lands or
  counter their threats first.
