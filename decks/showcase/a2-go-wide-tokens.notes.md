# A2 Go-wide Tokens: Notes

Part of the [archetype showcase](ARCHETYPES.md). Shared rules and power band
live there.

## Goals

- Fill the board with 1/1 tokens, pump them all at once, and swing with the
  whole team.
- Wins around turns 5 to 6.

## Constraints

- Orzhov (WB). Every card makes tokens or pumps them.
- No exclusions.

## Primer

### Game Plan

Spread out first, pump second. Fill the board with 1/1 tokens, then use
anthems and Hero of Bladehold to swing with the whole team. An anthem with
no tokens does nothing, so do not cast one onto an empty board. Wins around
turns 5 to 6.

### Key Synergies

- **Anthem stack:** Intangible Virtue, Glorious Anthem, and Sorin, Solemn
  Visitor stack, so each extra token adds more damage.
- **Flying tokens:** Bitterblossom, Lingering Souls, and Spectral
  Procession make flyers that ignore ground blockers.
- **Battle cry and tokens:** Hero of Bladehold makes two attacking
  Soldiers each combat, which battle cry then pumps.
- **Zealous Persecution:** one card pumps your team and shrinks theirs, so
  it can win combat and kill X/1 blockers.

### Token Makers

- **Raise the Alarm:** an instant that makes two 1/1 Soldiers. Cast it at
  the end of their turn or as surprise blockers.
- **Bitterblossom:** at your upkeep you lose 1 life and make a 1/1
  flying Faerie Rogue. Best on turn 1 or 2; the life loss adds up in long
  games.
- **Lingering Souls:** makes two 1/1 flying Spirits. Flash it back for
  {1}{B} to make two more, so four flyers from one card.
- **Spectral Procession:** makes three 1/1 flying Spirits. Its cost has
  hybrid white mana, so it is cheapest with white sources.
- **Secure the Wastes:** an instant that makes X 1/1 Warriors. Cast it at
  the end of their turn with spare mana, or during combat as blockers.

### Anthems and Pump

- **Intangible Virtue:** creature tokens you control get +1/+1 and have
  vigilance. Only tokens benefit, which is why the deck is token-heavy.
- **Glorious Anthem:** creatures you control get +1/+1. Cast it after you
  have three or more creatures.
- **Zealous Persecution:** an instant that gives your creatures +1/+1 and
  your opponents' creatures -1/-1 until end of turn. Use it in combat to
  win a block or wipe small blockers.
- **Sorin, Solemn Visitor:** +1 gives your creatures +1/+0 and lifelink until
  your next turn. -2 makes a 2/2 flying Vampire. Use the +1 right before a
  big attack.

### Top End

- **Hero of Bladehold:** a 3/4 with battle cry. Whenever it attacks, it makes
  two 1/1 Soldiers tapped and attacking. Cast it on turn 4 and protect it
  for one attack.
- **Elspeth, Sun's Champion:** +1 makes three 1/1 Soldiers. -3 destroys all
  creatures with power 4 or greater, which is a defense against big
  blockers. Cast it when you are behind on board.

### Mana

- **Godless Shrine:** a shock land that taps for W or B. Pay 2 life if you
  want it untapped.
- **Concealed Courtyard:** a land that taps for W or B and enters untapped
  if you control two or fewer other lands. Play it early.
- **Caves of Koilos:** taps for colorless, or W or B for 1 damage to you.
- **Shambling Vent:** enters tapped, taps for W or B, and can become a 2/3
  lifelink creature for {1}{W}{B}. A late-game mana sink.
- **Plains (8) and Swamp (3):** basic lands. White is the main color, so
  there are more Plains.

### Mulligan

Keep hands with 2 to 4 lands and a one- or two-mana token maker. A hand
with Bitterblossom or Raise the Alarm is a good keep. Mulligan hands of all
anthems and no token makers.

### Turn Guide

1. Turn 1: Bitterblossom, or a land if your hand is slow.
2. Turn 2: Raise the Alarm, or Intangible Virtue onto a token if you have
   Bitterblossom out.
3. Turn 3: Lingering Souls or Spectral Procession, plus an anthem if you
   have the mana.
4. Turn 4: Hero of Bladehold, or Secure the Wastes at the end of their
   turn.
5. Turns 5 to 6: add Glorious Anthem or Elspeth, Sun's Champion, flash
   back Lingering Souls, and attack with the full team. Use Zealous
   Persecution or Sorin, Solemn Visitor to push lethal.

### Matchups and Cautions

- **Good against:** decks that kill one creature at a time (Rock, Voltron).
- **Struggles against:** board sweepers (Tap-out control) and Group slug.
- Hold back an extra token maker after your first board is out so a
  sweeper does not leave you with nothing.
- Bitterblossom's life loss makes racing Burn and other aggro decks risky.
